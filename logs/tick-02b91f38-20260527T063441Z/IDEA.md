# Game Idea: Space Debris Dodge

- **Genre**: Endless survival / arcade.
- **Core loop**: Player rotates a spaceship and thrusts to avoid incoming asteroids that scroll down the canvas.
- **Controls**: Arrow keys or WASD.
- **Goal**: Survive as long as possible, collect occasional power‑ups for score boost.
- **Lose condition**: Collision with an asteroid or moving beyond the canvas edges ends the game.
