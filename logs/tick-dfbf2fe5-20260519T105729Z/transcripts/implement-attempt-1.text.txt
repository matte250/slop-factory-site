Implemented **Asteroid Sprint** game in `game.js` targeting `<canvas id="game">`.

- Sets up full‑screen canvas and main loop.
- Ship with thrust, gravity, and boundary handling.
- Random asteroids and occasional fuel cells spawn and move left.
- Collision detection ends the game; fuel collection slightly extends playtime.
- Keyboard (Space) and mouse click input for thrust.
- Simple UI shows elapsed time and “Game Over” overlay.
