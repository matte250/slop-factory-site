## Concept
The player controls a glowing dot that auto‑advances along a sinusoidal wave; each tap shifts the wave’s phase, moving the dot up or down to weave through vertical gaps.

## Player
A small, bright dot or orb representing the rider.  It constantly moves forward along the wave.  Core verbs: **auto‑ride** (the dot progresses automatically), **phase‑shift** (tap to move the wave up or down, steering the dot), and **restart** (reset after a loss).

## Core loop
Every 1–3 seconds the player watches the wave scroll forward, sees the next gap, and decides whether to tap to shift the wave upward or downward. The wave phase change takes effect immediately, moving the dot to a new vertical position. Successfully threading through a gap rewards the player and the wave continues; a mistimed tap leads to collision.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Vertical gaps**: sections of the wave where the solid barrier is missing; missing a gap results in a collision.
- **Static spikes**: fixed protrusions that hang from the wave crest or trough; contacting them ends the run.
- **Moving sweepers**: horizontal bars that sweep across the wave’s path at predictable intervals, requiring timing to avoid.
- **Laser beams**: thin, pulsing lines that appear briefly across the wave, demanding precise phase timing.

## Score
- **Gap passed**: each successfully navigated gap increments the score.
- **Pickup collected**: occasional floating icons (e.g., neon orbs) grant bonus points when the dot passes through them.
- **Combo chain**: consecutive gap passes without a miss increase a multiplier that boosts the points earned per gap.

## Win and lose
- **Lose**: collision with any obstacle or missing a gap; the screen flashes, displays “Game Over” with the final score, and prompts the player to restart.
- **Win**: reaching a predefined distance or number of gaps (e.g., completing the final wave segment) triggers a celebratory overlay with “You Win!” and the final score.

## Feel
A steady yet escalating pace: the wave’s rhythm is hypnotic, the neon‑lit visuals on a dark background create a futuristic vibe, and subtle chiptune beeps punctuate each successful tap and score event. The experience is both meditative (riding the wave) and adrenaline‑pumping (tight timing).

## Why it's fun
The thrill of precisely timing a tap to shift the wave and thread a perfect line through ever‑narrowing gaps keeps players on the edge of their seats.
