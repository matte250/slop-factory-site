## Concept
The player controls a forward‑moving dot racing through a three‑lane tunnel, tapping to instantly shift the dot left or right (wrapping around) to line up with gaps and avoid obstacles. The satisfaction comes from rapid decision‑making and feeling the momentum of the tunnel while threading through ever‑changing openings.

## Player
Avatar: a small glowing dot representing the player's craft.
Verbs: shift left, shift right, (optional) pause. The dot continuously moves forward; only lane changes affect survival.

## Core loop
Every moment the tunnel scrolls forward, presenting a sequence of patterned gaps in each of the three lanes. The player watches the upcoming lane pattern, then taps left or right to move the dot into the safe lane before the gap reaches the dot. Successful alignment yields a reward and the tunnel continues; mistiming results in collision and loss.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- Static barriers: solid blocks that fill an entire lane segment, requiring the player to be in a different lane.
- Moving shutters: temporary closing walls that sweep across a lane and then retract, forcing quick lane changes.
- Gap traps: narrow openings that appear only briefly, encouraging precise timing.
All obstacles appear in any lane and may appear simultaneously in multiple lanes, but never block all three lanes at once.

## Score
- Each survived gap increments the score.
- Bonus points for chaining consecutive safe passes without shifting (maintaining momentum).
- Collectible pulses (optional) that appear in safe lanes grant extra points when the dot passes through them.

## Win and lose
The player loses when the dot collides with any barrier or fails to be in a lane with an opening at the moment the obstacle reaches the dot. The player wins by reaching a predefined distance or number of obstacles (e.g., the tunnel's end) and a final "victory" screen appears, showing the score and a prompt to restart.

## Feel
Fast‑paced, rhythm‑driven arcade action with a neon‑glow aesthetic. The tunnel glows with pulsing colors and the dot leaves a subtle trailing light. Sound consists of crisp electronic beeps for lane shifts and satisfying chimes for successful passes, creating a tense yet upbeat atmosphere.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of instantly swapping lanes to thread a moving dot through ever‑shifting gaps generates those “got‑me!” moments that make players grin or curse in equal measure.
