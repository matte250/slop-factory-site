# Game Idea: Asteroid Escape

- **Genre**: Top‑down endless dodge
- **Controls**: Arrow keys or WASD to steer a small ship
- **Gameplay**: Random asteroids spawn from screen edges and drift across the canvas. Collect occasional fuel cells to keep the timer running.
- **Lose Condition**: Collision with any asteroid or running out of fuel/time.
- **Goal**: Survive as long as possible and achieve a high score.
