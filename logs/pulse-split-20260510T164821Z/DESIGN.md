## Concept
A lone dot constantly moves forward, and each tap spawns a fleeting clone that runs alongside for a short stretch, letting you weave one through a gap while the original takes a different path before they reunite.

## Player
The player controls a simple glowing dot that auto‑advances along a linear path. **Verbs:** tap to create a temporary clone, release to let the clone fade, merge to reunite the two dots.

## Core loop
The dot marches forward automatically. The player watches the upcoming pattern of gaps and obstacles, then taps at just the right moment to split the dot. One of the two strands threads through a narrow opening while the other bypasses a different hazard. After a brief distance, the strands merge and the cycle repeats. The tension comes from timing the split and choosing which strand takes which route.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Static barriers**: solid walls that block the dot’s path; one of the split strands must go around it.
- **Moving sweepers**: horizontal or vertical bars that sweep across the lane, forcing the player to split and send one strand through the safe side.
- **Pulse spikes**: short‑lived expanding circles that appear after a delay; a split allows one strand to slip through the safe gap while the other waits.
- **Branching forks**: brief diverging paths where only one branch leads forward; the player must decide which split strand to send down each fork.

## Score
- Successful merge after a split.
- Passing through a gap without collision.
- Collecting brief glowing pickups that appear on the path of either strand.
- Completing a series of consecutive splits without a miss (combo bonus).

## Win and lose
**Win:** Reach the end of a level’s designed sequence, triggering a satisfying visual convergence of the dot’s trail and a celebratory burst.
**Lose:** Any strand collides with a hazard, causing the dot to shatter; the screen flashes and a short “game over” tone plays.

## Feel
A steady, rhythmic pace that ramps into frantic bursts when multiple obstacles line up. Visuals are minimalistic neon on a dark background, with smooth trailing lines. Audio features soft synth pulses for movement and crisp, echoing blips for taps and merges, creating an immersive, almost synesthetic flow.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of instant, split‑second decision‑making—splitting the dot just in time to thread one through a tight gap while the other darts past a danger—delivers that perfect mix of tension and payoff.
