Implemented **`game.js`** with a concise Meteor Dodge arcade game:

- Targets the existing `<canvas id="game">`.
- Handles ship movement via arrow/W‑S keys.
- Spawns meteors and fuel pickups with random timing and speed.
- Performs collision detection, timer, fuel management, and game‑over logic.
- Renders a minimal starfield, ship (green triangle), meteors (red squares), fuel (yellow squares), and UI overlay.
