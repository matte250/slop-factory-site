Added audio support:

* **Background music** – loops silently until the first player movement, then starts.
* **Thrust sound** – plays each time the ship moves.
* **Crash sound** – plays on collision/game‑over.

Implemented by creating `Audio` objects, managing a `audioStarted` flag, and triggering the sounds inside the game loop. The new audio assets are royalty‑free URLs and respect volume levels.
