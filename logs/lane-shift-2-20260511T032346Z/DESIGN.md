## Concept
The player guides a constantly‑forward moving dot through an endless tunnel by tapping to shift all obstacles left or right, lining up with the open lane. The pleasure comes from quick, decisive lane shifts that keep the dot racing forward.

## Player
The avatar is a bright dot (or tiny ship) that always moves forward through the tunnel.
Verbs: shift (tap left/right to slide obstacles), dodge (avoid colliding lanes), keep moving (maintain forward momentum).

## Core loop
Every few seconds a new pattern of obstacles scrolls toward the player. The player reads the upcoming pattern, decides whether to tap left or right to shift the entire pattern, executes the tap, and the tunnel scrolls forward while the dot races through the newly aligned gap. This rapid read‑act‑react cycle repeats, driving momentum and tension.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- Solid lane blocks that fill one or two lanes for a short stretch, forcing a lane shift to survive.
- Dual‑lane walls that leave only a single lane open, demanding precise timing.
- Horizontal sweepers that travel across lanes within a segment, creating a moving barrier that must be avoided by shifting at the right moment.
- Burst spikes that appear briefly in a lane before vanishing, adding an extra timing challenge.

## Score
The score rises each time the player successfully passes an obstacle segment without collision. Bonus points are awarded for perfect timing when a shift aligns exactly with a narrow gap.

## Win and lose
The player loses instantly on any collision with an obstacle; a “Game Over” overlay appears showing the final score and restart instructions. The player wins by surviving a predefined distance or time, triggering a celebratory “You Win!” screen with a burst of neon.

## Feel
Frantic, high‑velocity arcade action with a neon‑lit tunnel that pulses to synth music. The aesthetic is sharp, glowing neon against a dark backdrop, with crisp sound effects for each shift and collision.

## Why it's fun
The instant, satisfying snap of shifting lanes to thread a dot through ever‑tighter gaps creates a rush that makes players grin, curse, or shout with each close call.
