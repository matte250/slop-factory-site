# Game Idea: Space Junk Collector

Control a small spaceship using the mouse or arrow keys to collect floating space debris for points. Dangerous asteroids drift across the screen; colliding with one ends the game. The ship also has a limited fuel gauge that depletes over time—running out of fuel also triggers a loss. The objective is to gather as many debris pieces as possible before losing.

**Lose Conditions**
- Collision with any asteroid.
- Fuel reaches zero.
