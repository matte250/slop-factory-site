**Added sound effects**

- Created a single `AudioContext` and a reusable `playTone(freq, duration)` helper.
- Added a resume check for the audio context on any key press.
- Played a short 800 Hz tone when the player jumps.
- Played a short 400 Hz tone when the player slides.

All other game logic and graphics remain unchanged. The game now provides audible feedback for the two core actions.
