## Concept

A continuously advancing dot can be toggled between a small and a large radius. The player times these toggles to thread the dot through gaps that only accept one size, creating a rhythmic puzzle of expansion and contraction.

## Player

The player controls a single circular avatar that moves forward automatically. The only player action is tapping or clicking to switch the dot’s radius between a compact and an expanded state.

## Core loop

Every 1–3 seconds a new segment of the path appears with a gap sized for either the small or the large dot. The player watches the upcoming gap, decides whether to stay the same size or toggle, and the dot reaches the gap automatically. Passing safely yields reward; mismatching size results in collision.

## Hazards / Obstacles

- **Size‑specific openings**: solid barriers with a single cutout that fits only the small or only the large dot.
- **Dynamic squeezers**: moving slabs that slide across the path, closing the space for one size while leaving the other open.
- **Pulse walls**: barriers that expand and contract on a timer, requiring the player to match the current size to slip through.

## Score

- +1 point for each gap successfully traversed.
- Bonus points for consecutive correct size matches without error (combo multiplier).
- Optional achievement points for reaching milestone gaps (e.g., every 10th gap).

## Win and lose

- **Lose**: Colliding with any barrier ends the game instantly; a "Game Over" overlay appears with the final score.
- **Win**: Surviving a predefined sequence of gaps (e.g., thirty) triggers a "You Win!" celebration screen and final score.

## Feel

Steady, flowing pace that gradually tightens as gaps become more frequent. Minimalist neon aesthetic with a dark background and bright cyan/magenta accents. Subtle chiptune blips accompany each size toggle and each successful pass, while a soft thud signals a collision.

## Why it's fun

The thrill of perfectly timing a size change to slip through a tight opening delivers that instant "aha!" moment of mastery.
