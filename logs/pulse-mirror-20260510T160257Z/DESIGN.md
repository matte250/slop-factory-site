## Concept
The player guides a moving dot through a tunnel while timing taps to reflect expanding pulses, using mirrored waves to clear barriers and progress.

## Player
The player controls a glowing dot that constantly advances forward through a tunnel. Verbs: tap (to reflect the pulse), observe (the pulse expansion), and steer (minor lateral adjustments if needed).

## Core loop
Each pulse expands outward from the dot, and the player must decide when to tap to reflect it back before it reaches the tunnel walls. A reflected pulse travels backward, toggling the state of opposite‑side barriers, opening a path. The player reads the visual cue of the pulse radius, chooses the optimal tap moment, and moves forward to the newly cleared corridor.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Static barriers**: Solid walls that block the tunnel until a mirrored pulse toggles them off.
- **Moving sweepers**: Horizontal hazards that sweep across the tunnel, requiring timing to avoid when the pulse is reflected.
- **Pulse‑absorbing nodes**: Points that nullify a pulse if it reaches them unreflected, forcing the player to react faster.

## Score
- Reflecting a pulse that clears a barrier.
- Successfully navigating past a moving sweeper.
- Completing a segment of the tunnel without a missed tap.

## Win and lose
- **Win**: Reach the end of the tunnel after a set number of segments, with the final pulse reflected to open the exit.
- **Lose**: Collide with a barrier, sweeper, or allow a pulse to hit a pulse‑absorbing node. The screen flashes, and the dot fades out.

## Feel
A steady yet tense rhythm with a neon‑glow aesthetic, combining smooth synth ambience and subtle arcade beeps. The visual style is clean, minimalist, and slightly retro, encouraging focus on timing and flow.

## Why it's fun
The satisfying moment when a perfectly timed tap sends a pulse racing back, instantly clearing a wall and revealing a fresh path.
