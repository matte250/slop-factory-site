## Game Idea: "Astro Dodge"

A minimalist HTML Canvas game where the player controls a small spaceship that automatically moves forward through an endless field of asteroids. The ship can steer left/right and boost upward to avoid incoming rocks. The background scrolls to simulate motion.

**Goal**: Survive as long as possible, racking up distance points.

**Lose Condition**: The game ends when the spaceship collides with any asteroid or moves off the visible canvas area.
