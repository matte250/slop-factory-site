Added a lightweight sound system:

* **Audio context** and helper `playTone(freq, type, duration)` for one‑shot tones.  
* **Background hum** (low‑volume 60 Hz sine) that starts after the first user click (browser‑required interaction).  
* **Crystal collection** plays a short 800 Hz triangle tone.  
* **Collision / game‑over** plays a 200 Hz sawtooth tone, stops the hum, and marks the end of the game.  

All audio is generated with the Web Audio API, so no external files are needed. The game now has ambient background sound, feedback for collection, and an audible cue when the player loses.
