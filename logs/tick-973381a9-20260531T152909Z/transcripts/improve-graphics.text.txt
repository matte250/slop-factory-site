**Graphics enhancements added**

- Background gradient with dark space tones.
- Starfield (100 random stars) for a richer backdrop.
- Planet now uses a radial gradient for a glassy appearance.
- Ship rendered as a triangle oriented along its orbit direction.
- Bullets changed to small white sparks.
- Asteroids rendered with radial gradients for a rocky look.
- Canvas resize now recalculates center and asteroid spawn distance.

The game now has a more polished visual style while preserving existing gameplay.
