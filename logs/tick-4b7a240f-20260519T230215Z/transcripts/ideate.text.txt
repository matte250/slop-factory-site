# Game Idea: **Orbit Avoider**

A minimalist HTML canvas game where the player pilots a small spaceship orbiting a planet. The ship automatically revolves around the planet, and the player can thrust radially inward or outward to collect floating power‑ups while avoiding incoming asteroids.  
**Lose condition:** collision with an asteroid or drifting off‑screen (leaving the orbit).  

The design uses simple controls, basic physics, and canvas drawing, making it straightforward to implement.
