Implemented **Orbit Avoider** game in `game.js`:

- Targets `<canvas id="game">`.
- Handles ship orbit physics, radial thrust (↑ inward, ↓ outward).
- Spawns power‑ups (collect for score) and asteroids (collision ends game).
- Displays planet, ship, items, score, and game‑over overlay.
- Uses simple vector math, keyboard input, and requestAnimationFrame loop.
