## Concept
The player taps to instantly flip the direction of a rolling dot, using momentum to bounce between alternating walls that only open on one side, creating a rhythm of timed reversals and tight navigation.

## Player
The player controls a simple dot that constantly rolls forward with inertia. **Verbs:** tap to reverse velocity direction instantly.

## Core loop
The dot rolls forward until the player taps, instantly reversing its velocity while keeping speed. The player watches the approaching wall, times the tap to avoid collision, and rides the dot back toward the opposite wall, repeating the rhythm every few seconds.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **One‑way walls:** walls that only allow passage from one side; hitting the closed side ends the run.
- **Moving barriers:** panels that slide into the path of the dot, requiring precise tap timing to evade.
- **Timed shutters:** sections that open briefly on one side, forcing the player to sync flips with the opening window.

## Score
- Each successful bounce off a wall adds points.
- Flipping exactly when a moving barrier passes awards a bonus.
- Completing a series of consecutive flawless flips without a miss grants a multiplier.

## Win and lose
- **Win:** reach a predefined number of consecutive perfect flips or achieve a target score, triggering a celebratory burst of light and sound.
- **Lose:** collide with a closed side of a wall or a moving barrier, causing the dot to stop and a brief “game over” flash.

## Feel
Fast‑paced yet minimalist, with a neon‑glow aesthetic and crisp arcade‑style sound effects. The gameplay feels tight and rhythmic, encouraging quick reflexes while maintaining a clear visual focus.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of instantly flipping momentum at the last possible instant, turning a simple tap into a satisfying, high‑stakes dance with the walls.
