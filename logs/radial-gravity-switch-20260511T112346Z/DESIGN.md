## Concept
A solitary dot continuously travels forward through a looping circular tunnel. Tapping toggles the central gravity, pulling the dot inward or pushing it outward, letting the player drift between inner and outer rings to thread through gaps.

## Player
The player controls a single glowing dot that automatically moves along the tunnel’s forward direction. Its only actions are toggling gravity (inward vs outward) via tap or click.

## Core loop
The dot advances automatically around the tunnel while the player watches upcoming gaps. Each time a gap approaches, the player decides whether to pull the dot toward the center or push it outward, then taps to switch gravity. Successful navigation through a gap yields a small reward and the tunnel continues; mistimed switches cause collision with a ring segment.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Rotating ring segments**: Fixed arcs that leave gaps; colliding with a solid segment ends the game.
- **Moving barriers**: Short radial spikes that travel along the tunnel and require quick gravity switches to avoid.
- **Laser arcs**: Pulsing beams that appear on either the inner or outer ring, demanding the dot stay on the opposite side.

## Score
Score increments each time the dot successfully passes through a gap, with bonus points for consecutive gap passes without a miss. Collectible “energy pulses” appear occasionally; grabbing one adds a score burst.

## Win and lose
The player loses when the dot collides with any solid ring segment, barrier, or laser. On loss the screen shows “Game Over” with the final score and a prompt to restart. The game has no explicit win condition; instead high scores encourage replay, but a “perfect run” (clearing a preset number of gaps) triggers a “You Win!” celebration.

## Feel
A rhythmically pulsing, neon‑glow aesthetic with a steady yet tense tempo. The visual style is dark with bright cyan and magenta rings, and the audio is a minimal synth pulse that rises with each successful switch. Gameplay feels tight and reactive, rewarding precise timing.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of instantly switching gravity to slip through tight, moving gaps creates a satisfying “just‑in‑time” moment that makes players grin or gasp with each successful drift.
