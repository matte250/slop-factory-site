Implemented **Canvas Escape** game per the idea in `IDEA.md`.

- Added `game.js` targeting the `<canvas id="game">`.
- Features:
  - Player triangle controls: Arrow ←/→ to steer, Arrow ↑ to jump.
  - Endless scrolling obstacles (rectangles) with simple AABB collision.
  - Game‑over overlay with “Press R to Restart”.
  - Responsive canvas sizing based on element dimensions.
- Self‑contained IIFE, no external dependencies.
