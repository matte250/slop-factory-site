Added simple sound effects:

- Created an `AudioContext` and a `playBeep` helper that generates short square‑wave tones.
- Resumes the audio context on the first key press (`audioStarted` guard).
- Plays a brief beep each time the ship moves.
- Plays a longer, lower‑pitch beep when a collision ends the game.

All changes are in **game.js** and require no external assets. The game now has immediate audio feedback for movement and game‑over events.
