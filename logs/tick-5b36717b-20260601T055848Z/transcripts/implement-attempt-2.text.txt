Implemented a concise **Neon Wave** game in `game.js` targeting the canvas with `id="game"`.

- Sets up canvas dimensions.
- Handles ship movement (left/right) with arrow keys.
- Spawns upward‑moving obstacles at intervals.
- Detects collisions and displays “Game Over” when the ship hits an obstacle or falls off the bottom.
- Uses `requestAnimationFrame` for smooth rendering.
