# Game Idea: Cosmic Runner

**Overview**: A fast‑paced endless runner where the player pilots a spaceship across a scrolling starfield.

**Core Gameplay**:
- The ship automatically moves forward; the player can steer left/right and use a boost.
- Asteroids and space debris appear from the right; the player must dodge them.
- Floating fuel cells appear; collecting them increases score and extends fuel.

**Lose Condition**:
- Collision with any asteroid or debris.
- Fuel runs out (fuel depletes over time and when boosting).

**Canvas Feasibility**: Simple 2‑D sprites, particle background, and collision detection can be implemented with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript.
