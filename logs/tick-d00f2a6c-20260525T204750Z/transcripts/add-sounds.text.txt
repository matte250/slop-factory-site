Implemented sound effects:

- **Audio context** created with `AudioContext`.
- Helper `playTone` to generate simple sine‑wave tones.
- `playCollision` (low‑frequency hit sound) called on asteroid collision.
- `playBoost` (short higher‑pitch tone) triggered whenever the ship moves.
- Resumes the suspended audio context on the first key interaction.

All changes are in **game.js** and integrate seamlessly with the existing game loop.
