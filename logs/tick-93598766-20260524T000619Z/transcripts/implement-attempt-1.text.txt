Implemented **Orb Escape** game in `game.js`:

- Targets `<canvas id="game">` from the existing HTML.
- Handles player movement (arrow keys), orb spawning/movement/growth, coin collection, scoring, and loss condition.
- Includes resize handling, game‑over overlay, and restart (`R` key) functionality.
