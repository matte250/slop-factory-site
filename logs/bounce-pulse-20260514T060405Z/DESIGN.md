## Concept

The dot constantly moves forward; each tap makes it briefly leap upward, letting players time jumps to bounce over low obstacles or slip through high gaps in a rhythmic flow.

## Player

A neon-colored dot that auto‑advances horizontally. Verb: jump (short upward hop triggered by tap).

## Core loop

The dot glides forward while obstacles scroll towards it. Every moment the player decides whether to tap now to jump or wait for the next gap. Successful jumps let the dot clear hazards; mistimed taps cause collisions. Surviving each obstacle increments the score and the world speeds up, tightening the rhythm.

## Hazards / Obstacles

- Low barriers that extend from the ground and must be cleared by a jump.
- High arches that hang from the ceiling, requiring the dot to stay low (by not jumping) to pass.
- Moving vertical spikes that sweep horizontally, demanding precise timing to avoid.
- Floating gaps in the floor that appear periodically, inviting the player to dip by not jumping.

## Score

- Each obstacle successfully passed without collision.
- Collecting occasional glowing orbs placed in the path.
- Maintaining a streak of perfectly timed jumps (bonus points).

## Win and lose

- Lose: colliding with any obstacle ends the game immediately.
- Win: surviving a sustained period (e.g., reaching a set time or score threshold) triggers a celebratory "You Win!" overlay.

## Feel

The game feels like a pulsing rhythm game with a steady build‑up that turns frantic as speed rises. Neon graphics on a dark background give a retro‑futuristic vibe, while simple chiptune blips underscore each jump and collision.

## Why it's fun

Landing a perfectly timed tap that lets the dot glide through a tight gap delivers an instant, satisfying "aha!" moment that makes you want to keep the rhythm going.
