# Game Idea: Asteroid Escape

A simple HTML‑canvas arcade game where the player controls a small ship with arrow keys, navigating a field of randomly spawning asteroids that drift across the screen. The ship must avoid any collision; touching an asteroid immediately ends the game. The score increases over time, encouraging longer survival and occasional power‑ups for temporary shields.
