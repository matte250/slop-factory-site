Implemented **Neon Escape** game in `game.js`:

- Targets the existing `<canvas id="game">`.
- Handles player movement, glowing square rendering, and invincibility.
- Generates scrolling colored bars as obstacles and occasional shield power‑ups.
- Includes collision detection, speed increase over time, and a game‑over overlay.
- Listens for arrow‑key input and runs a requestAnimationFrame loop.
