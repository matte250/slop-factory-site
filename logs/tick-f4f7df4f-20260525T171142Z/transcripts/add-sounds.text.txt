Added audio support:

- Created an `AudioContext` with helper `playSound`, plus specific `playCatchSound` and `playGameOverSound` functions.  
- Resumes the audio context on the first key press (required by browsers).  
- Plays a short catch‑tone when a star is collected.  

All changes are in **game.js**, which still targets the `<canvas id="game">`. The game now has sound effects while keeping the enhanced graphics.
