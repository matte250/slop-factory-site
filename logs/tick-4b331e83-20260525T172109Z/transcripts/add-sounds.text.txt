Added simple sound effects using the Web Audio API:

* **Jump** – high‑pitched synth tone.
* **Orb collection** – brighter synth tone.
* **Collision / Game Over** – low, longer tone.

Integrated audio initialization and a `playSynth(freq, dur)` helper, and wired the calls into the existing player jump, orb collection, and obstacle collision logic. All changes are in `game.js`.
