Implemented **Neon Runner** game in `game.js`:

* Targets the `<canvas id="game">` element.
* Handles player movement, jumping, gravity, and ground detection.
* Spawns scrolling obstacles and collectible orbs.
* Detects collisions, updates score, and displays a game‑over overlay.
* Supports keyboard (Space) and mouse/tap input.
* Uses simple neon color scheme and automatic canvas sizing.
