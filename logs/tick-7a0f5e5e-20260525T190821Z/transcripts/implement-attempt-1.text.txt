Implemented **game.js** with a concise side‑scrolling endless runner:

- Targets the canvas with `id="game"`.
- Handles player jump, gravity, and ground detection.
- Spawns obstacles and optional stars, updates positions, and checks collisions.
- Renders player, obstacles, stars, ground, score, and a game‑over overlay.
- Supports mouse and touch input for jumping.
- Starts the game loop and initial spawn.
