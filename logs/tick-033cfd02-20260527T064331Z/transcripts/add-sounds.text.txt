Added simple sound effects:

* **Audio setup** – created an `AudioContext` with helper `playTone`, `playThrust`, and `playCollision`.
* **Thrust sound** – plays a short 200 Hz tone whenever the up‑arrow is held (resumes the audio context if it’s suspended).
* **Collision sounds** – plays a lower 100 Hz tone when the ship hits the planet or a drone, guarded so the sound fires only once per game‑over event.
* Integrated the sound calls into the existing update loop.

The game now has audible feedback for thrust and collisions while preserving the existing gameplay and graphics.
