**Pixel Escape**  
A minimalist HTML canvas game where the player controls a small square that continuously moves forward through a field of randomly generated obstacles. The player can move up/down/left/right to dodge. Colliding with any obstacle ends the game (lose condition). The goal is to survive as long as possible while accumulating points for each obstacle passed. Simple shapes and keyboard controls make it easy to implement.
