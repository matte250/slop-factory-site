Implemented a richer visual experience:

* **Twinkling starfield** – stationary stars with random opacity changes.  
* **Gradient‑shaded ship** – vertical gradient gives depth.  
* **Mineral glow** – radial gradient for a gold‑ish look.  
* **Rotating, polygon‑shaped asteroids** – each has its own rotation speed and irregular shape.  
* **Explosion particles** – colorful bursts appear on collisions.  
* **Shield arc** – a semi‑transparent blue ring around the ship that fades with shield level.  
* **Background & HUD colors** – darker space backdrop (`#001`), brighter HUD text, and updated game‑over overlay.  
* Updated render loop to draw stars, entities, explosions, and HUD in the proper order.

All changes are in `game.js` and run automatically on the existing `<canvas id="game">`. The game now has a much more polished and dynamic look while keeping the original mechanics.
