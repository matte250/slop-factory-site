Added sound effects:

- **Audio context** with helper `playBeep`.
- **Collect sound** triggered when a mineral is collected.
- **Explosion sound** triggered on asteroid collisions.
- **Background ambient music** (`space-ambient.ogg`) that starts on the first user interaction (key press or click) and loops.

All changes are within `game.js`, integrating seamlessly with the enhanced graphics. The game now has both visual and audio feedback.
