# Random Canvas Game Idea

**Title:** *Orbit Runner*

**Concept:** Control a spaceship that constantly drifts forward on an HTML canvas. The player can rotate and thrust to avoid incoming asteroids and collect fuel cells. The background scrolls to give a sense of motion.

**Lose Condition:** Colliding with an asteroid or running out of fuel ends the game.

**Goal:** Survive as long as possible and achieve a high score.
