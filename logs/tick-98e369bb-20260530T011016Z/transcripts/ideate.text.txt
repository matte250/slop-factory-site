**IDEA.md** has been created with the following concise game concept:

**Orbit Defender**

- Player controls a rotating shield (orbit) around a central planet using left/right arrows.  
- Asteroids spawn at the edges and travel straight toward the planet.  
- The shield blocks asteroids; each blocked asteroid protects the planet.  
- Missed asteroids damage the planet’s health (starting at 3).  
- **Lose condition:** game ends when the planet’s health reaches zero.
