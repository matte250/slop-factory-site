Implemented **IDEA.md** with a concise HTML‑canvas game concept:

- **Title:** Meteor Dodge  
- **Genre:** Endless runner / survival  
- **Core mechanic:** Ship moves left/right at canvas bottom, dodging falling meteors.  
- **Goal:** Survive as long as possible.  
- **Lose condition:** Collision between any meteor and the ship ends the game.  
- **Scoring:** Time‑based points, with increasing meteor speed/frequency.  
- **Implementation notes:** Uses `requestAnimationFrame`, simple shape collision, and optional sound effects.
