**IDEA.md** created with a concise HTML canvas game concept:

**Title:** *Asteroid Dodge*  
**Concept:** Pilot a spaceship that must avoid asteroids scrolling from right to left. The player moves with arrow keys or WASD while the background scrolls to give a sense of forward motion.  
**Lose Condition:** Collision with an asteroid or moving off‑screen ends the game.  
**Goal:** Survive as long as possible; the score rises with time.
