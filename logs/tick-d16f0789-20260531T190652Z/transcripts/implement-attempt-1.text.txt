Implemented **game.js** with a concise Arcade “Orbit Escape” game:

- Targets `<canvas id="game">` and resizes to window.
- Ship centered, controllable via arrow keys, consumes fuel.
- Asteroids orbit the center; collision ends the game.
- Fuel pickups spawn periodically and increase score.
- HUD displays fuel and score, with a game‑over overlay.

The game follows the mechanics described in `IDEA.md`.
