Added a simple audio system:

- Created an `AudioContext` and `playTone` helper for short beep sounds.
- Resumes audio on first key press.
- Plays a crash tone when the ship hits an asteroid.
- Plays a collect tone when fuel canisters are gathered.

The game now has basic sound effects for collisions and pickups. Include the updated `game.js` in your HTML (`<script src="game.js"></script>`) to hear the sounds during play.
