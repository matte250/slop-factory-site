Added audio to the endless‑runner:

* **Sound assets** – `jump.wav`, `hit.wav`, and looping background music `bg.mp3` are loaded at start (you must provide these files or adjust paths). Background music plays automatically on a loop with low volume.
* **Jump sound** – played each time the player jumps.
* **Hit sound** – triggered once when a collision ends the game.
* Updated the collision block to play the hit sound only on the first collision.

The rest of the game logic, graphics, and controls remain unchanged. Load the updated `game.js` after the canvas element and ensure the audio files are accessible from the same directory (or adjust the paths). Enjoy the enhanced audio experience!
