Added **sound effects** using the Web Audio API:

- Initialized `AudioContext` and resumed it on first user interaction.
- Played a short beep on collisions (`playCollisionSound`).
- Played a subtle whoosh when meteors spawn (`playSpawnSound`).
- Played a brief tone when a meteor is avoided and the score increments (`playScoreSound`).
- Integrated these sounds into the game loop (spawn, score, and collision events).
