# Game Idea: Asteroid Escape

A minimal HTML canvas game where the player controls a small ship at the bottom of the screen, moving left and right to dodge falling asteroids. Asteroids spawn at random positions and speeds from the top and fall toward the bottom. The player can collect occasional power‑ups for temporary shields or speed boosts. **Lose condition:** The game ends immediately when the ship collides with any asteroid.

Goal: Survive as long as possible and achieve the highest score.
