**Game Idea: Asteroid Escape**

A simple HTML‑canvas game where the player pilots a tiny ship at the bottom of the screen, moving left/right to dodge falling asteroids. Asteroids spawn randomly from the top with varied speeds. Occasionally, power‑ups appear offering temporary shields or speed boosts.

**Lose condition:** The game ends instantly when the ship collides with any asteroid.

**Goal:** Survive as long as possible and achieve the highest score.
