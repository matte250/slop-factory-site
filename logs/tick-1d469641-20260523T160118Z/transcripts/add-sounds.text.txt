Added sound effects:

* **Background rumble** – low‑frequency sine oscillator that starts on the first key press and runs continuously.
* **Thruster sound** – brief high‑pitched tone while the up‑arrow is held.
* **Collision sound** – short burst when the ship hits an asteroid (also stops the background rumble).  

All audio logic is initialized at the top of `game.js`, the audio context is resumed on the first interaction, and the appropriate `playTone` calls are inserted in the update loop and collision handler. The game now has basic auditory feedback while keeping the existing mechanics unchanged.
