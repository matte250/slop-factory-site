Implemented **Orbit Escape** game in `game.js`.  
- Targets `<canvas id="game">`.  
- Ship rotates with Arrow Left/Right, thrusts with Arrow Up, consumes fuel.  
- Rotating asteroids move across the canvas; colliding ends the game.  
- Fuel cells appear to replenish fuel; running out of fuel also ends the game.  
- Includes basic rendering, input handling, wrapping, and a simple HUD.
