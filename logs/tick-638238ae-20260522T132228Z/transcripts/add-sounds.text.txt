Added sound effects using the Web Audio API:

- **Audio setup** with a global `AudioContext`.
- `playTone(freq, dur)` helper to play simple tones.
- **Sounds**:
  * Cargo pickup – high‑pitch tone.
  * Successful delivery – mid‑pitch tone.
  * Asteroid collision – low‑pitch tone, plus a longer low tone on game‑over.
- Integrated sound calls into the relevant game events.
