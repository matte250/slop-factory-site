# Game Idea: Cosmic Courier

**Concept**: The player pilots a small spaceship through a scrolling starfield, collecting glowing cargo crates and delivering them to moving space stations.

**Gameplay**
- Arrow keys steer left/right and adjust speed.  
- Crates appear randomly; fly over them to collect.  
- Space stations drift horizontally; drop a crate when aligned to deliver and gain points.

**Lose Condition**  
- The ship has limited fuel that depletes over time and upon asteroid collisions.  
- The game ends when fuel reaches zero **or** the ship collides with three asteroids.

**Why It Works on HTML Canvas**  
- Uses simple geometric shapes and basic collision detection.  
- Scrolling background and moving objects provide engaging visuals while staying lightweight.
