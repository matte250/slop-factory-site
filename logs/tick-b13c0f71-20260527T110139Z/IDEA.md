# Game Idea

**Title:** Space Runner

**Concept:** A side‑scrolling endless runner where the player pilots a small spaceship that continuously moves forward through an asteroid field. The ship can boost up or down to avoid obstacles and collect floating energy orbs for points.

**Lose Condition:** Colliding with an asteroid or moving off the visible canvas bounds ends the game.

**Key Features:**
- Simple arrow or touch controls for vertical movement.
- Procedurally generated obstacles and power‑ups.
- Increasing speed and obstacle density over time for difficulty scaling.
- Score based on distance traveled and orbs collected.

**Why Canvas:** All graphics (ship, asteroids, background) can be drawn using 2D canvas shapes and images, with a game loop handling updates and collision detection.
