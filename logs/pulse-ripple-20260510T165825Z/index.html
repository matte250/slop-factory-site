## Concept
The player guides a moving dot forward, using rhythmic ripples to navigate through timed barriers; tapping creates a counter‑ripple that flips the wave, letting the dot slip through moments of safe passage.

## Player
The avatar is a simple glowing dot that continuously advances along a linear path.  Verbs: advance (auto‑forward), tap (emit secondary ripple), pause (optional, short hold to time a ripple), and dash (brief speed boost when ripple aligns).

## Core loop
Every few seconds the player watches the expanding primary ripple from the dot’s tail and anticipates incoming barriers that sync with the ripple’s phase. The player decides when to tap, generating a secondary ripple that temporarily reverses the wave direction, creating a brief window to pass through obstacles. Successive taps rhythmically chain ripples, keeping the flow and encouraging timing mastery.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Phase‑locked barriers**: walls that appear and disappear in sync with the primary ripple’s expansion, blocking the path unless the ripple is reversed.
- **Periodic spikes**: vertical spikes that pulse on the same rhythm as the wave; mistimed taps cause collision.
- **Moving shutters**: sliding panels that sweep across the path on a fixed period, visible cues indicate their travel direction.
- **Echo generators**: devices that emit a secondary wave of their own, potentially canceling the player’s ripple if not timed correctly.

## Score
- Successful passage through a barrier while the reversed ripple is active.
- Chain of consecutive safe passages without a miss (combo multiplier).
- Collecting optional luminescent orbs that appear briefly during ripple reversals.
- Completing a segment of the level without tapping (perfect timing).

## Win and lose
**Win**: Reach the end of the level after a set number of barrier sequences, triggering a final, harmonious crescendo of ripples that envelops the dot. **Lose**: Collide with a barrier, spike, or shutter; the screen flashes, the ripple collapses, and the dot fades out.

## Feel
A steady yet pulsating rhythm with neon‑glow visuals and smooth gradient backgrounds. The atmosphere feels futuristic and meditative, reinforced by a subtle synth‑wave soundtrack that rises and falls with each ripple.

## Why it's fun
Flipping the wave at just the right instant to watch the dot glide through an otherwise impassable barrier creates a satisfying, kinetic “aha!” moment.
