Implemented audio feedback:

* **Web‑Audio setup** – created an `AudioContext`, helper `playTone`, and sound‑effect functions `playThrust` and `playExplosion`.
* **Thrust sound** – called `playThrust()` each time a thrust particle is emitted while the ship moves.
* **Explosion sound** – called `playExplosion()` on collision/game over.

The game now produces a short square‑wave thrust beep and a deeper explosion tone, enhancing the experience while keeping the rest of the code unchanged.
