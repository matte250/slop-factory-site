Implemented **game.js** with a minimal endless‑runner based on the description in `IDEA.md`. The script:

- Targets the `<canvas id="game">` element.
- Sets up a three‑lane grid, a glowing player square, and randomly spawning blocks.
- Handles lane switching on click/tap, collision detection, scoring, and a 2‑minute timer.
- Renders the grid, player, blocks, and score each frame.
