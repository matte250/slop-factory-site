Implemented a concise **asteroid‑dodge game** in `game.js` that targets the `<canvas id="game">` element.

- Sets up canvas size and rendering context.  
- Handles ship movement via arrow keys, keeps it within bounds.  
- Spawns falling asteroids that increase speed with the score.  
- Detects collisions (ship vs. asteroid) and ends the game.  
- Updates score, draws ship (green triangle), asteroids (gray circles), and a game‑over overlay.  
- Runs a request‑animation‑frame loop for smooth gameplay.
