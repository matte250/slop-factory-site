## Concept
The player controls a dot that toggles between a tiny and a massive radius with each tap. The fun comes from matching the dot's size to the ever‑changing gaps in the obstacles, rewarding precise timing and quick decisions.

## Player
You are a luminous dot that can instantly switch its size. **Verbs:** tap/click to toggle size, move (drift) with inertia as the world scrolls, and possibly swipe to dash (optional). The avatar is a simple glowing circle.

## Core loop
Obstacles appear from the edges and move toward the player. Every few seconds a new pattern of gaps scrolls by, and the player must decide whether to stay small to slip through narrow passages or become large to ride through wide openings that would crush a small dot. Each toggle occurs on a tap, and the result is seen on the next frame, prompting rapid read‑and‑react cycles.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Narrow corridors:** tight vertical or horizontal gaps that only a small dot can pass.
- **Wide crushers:** large blocks that close in on a small dot but can be brushed past when the dot is large.
- **Rotating blades:** spinning arms that create alternating safe zones, requiring size changes to slip through the voids.
- **Pulsating walls:** obstacles that expand and contract, forcing the player to toggle size at the right moment.
All hazards move with a consistent rhythm but vary in pattern, never in absolute speed.

## Score
- **Survive an obstacle wave:** each completed set of gaps adds a point.
- **Perfect toggle:** switching size at the exact moment a gap opens grants a bonus.
- **Combo:** consecutive successful toggles without a miss multiplies the score increment.

## Win and lose
The player loses the moment the dot collides with any solid part of an obstacle while the size does not match the gap, causing an immediate game‑over overlay. The player wins by surviving a predetermined number of obstacle waves (the final wave features a spectacular cascade of alternating gaps). On win, the screen flashes with a burst of color and a “You Win!” message appears.

## Feel
A fast‑paced, rhythm‑driven arcade experience with a neon‑glow aesthetic on a dark background. Visuals pulse in time with the obstacles, and each toggle is accompanied by a sharp synth beep, reinforcing the timing feedback. The overall vibe is tense yet rewarding, urging the player to stay in the flow.

## Why it's fun
The single, satisfying moment when a perfectly timed size toggle lets the dot slip through a seemingly impossible gap, making the player grin or gasp with each close call.
