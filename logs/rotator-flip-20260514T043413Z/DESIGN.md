## Concept
The player guides a glowing dot through an endless tunnel filled with rotating blade obstacles, tapping to instantly reverse the blades' spin direction and line up the safe gaps for the dot to pass.

## Player
A small illuminated dot that constantly moves forward through the tunnel. The player can tap (or click) to flip the spin direction of all visible rotating blades. No other controls are required.

## Core loop
Each frame the dot advances forward while a series of rotating blades approach. The player watches the rotating gaps, decides whether to keep the current spin or flip it, and taps at the right moment. Successful alignment lets the dot slip through the gap; a miss results in a collision. New blades continuously spawn, increasing the rhythm and tension.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Rotating blades**: Circular obstacles with a single cut‑out gap. They spin continuously and can be reversed instantly by the player’s tap. The gap size is fixed, but its angle changes with spin direction.
- **Static spikes**: Fixed protrusions on the tunnel walls that the dot must avoid; they serve as visual variety and occasional forced timing adjustments.
- **Narrowing tunnel sections**: Sections where the corridor width shrinks, tightening the margin for error and making spin decisions more critical.

## Score
The score increments each time the dot successfully passes a rotating blade. Bonus points are awarded for consecutive flawless passes without flipping, encouraging risk‑taking and rhythm mastery.

## Win and lose
- **Lose**: The player loses the moment the dot collides with any blade, spike, or tunnel wall. A “Game Over” overlay displays the final score and restart instructions.
- **Win**: After surviving a predefined number of blades (e.g., 30) or reaching a target score, the player wins and sees a celebratory “You Win!” screen with final stats.

## Feel
Frantic and rhythmic, with a neon‑lit aesthetic that emphasizes fast, precise timing. The visual style is sleek, high‑contrast neon on a dark background, paired with a pulsing synth soundtrack and sharp sound cues for flips, passes, and collisions.

## Why it's fun
The visceral thrill of flipping a massive rotating blade at the last possible instant to thread a narrow gap makes players grin, curse, and keep trying for that perfect timing.
