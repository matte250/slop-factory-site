Added audio effects:

- Created an `AudioContext` and `playTone` helper for simple sine‑wave tones.  
- Initialized audio on the first key press (`audioStarted`).  
- Played a short movement tone (440 Hz) when the ship moves, throttled to ≤10 Hz.  
- Played a crash tone (150 Hz, longer) on collision.  

All changes are in `game.js`; the game now has sound feedback for movement and collisions.
