Implemented richer graphics:

- **Background** – vertical gradient (deep space) instead of solid black.  
- **Starfield** – a dynamic array of stars that twinkle and are drawn larger (2 px).  
- **Ship** – now rendered with a horizontal gradient for a subtle glow.  
- **Asteroids** – drawn as rotating irregular polygons for visual variety.  
- Added `stars` array and `initStars()` to populate it on canvas resize.  

All changes are in `game.js` and will enhance the visual experience while preserving existing gameplay.
