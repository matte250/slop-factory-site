**Game Idea – Orbit Dodger**

- **Core concept**: Pilot a ship that orbits a planet on an HTML canvas. Asteroids and enemy drones drift inward; the player must maneuver the ship to dodge them.
- **Lose condition**: The game ends instantly if the ship collides with any asteroid or is hit by enemy fire.
- **Scoring**: Track how long the player survives (time‑based score).
