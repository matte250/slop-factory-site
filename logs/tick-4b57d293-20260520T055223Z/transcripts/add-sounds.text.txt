Added sound effects using the Web Audio API:

* Created an `audioCtx` and a reusable `beep(freq, duration)` helper.
* Unlock audio on first key press and play a short move cue for every arrow‑key press.
* Played a low‑tone beep on game‑over (different tones for trap vs. timeout) and kept the existing alerts.
* Integrated the audio setup right after obtaining the canvas context.

All changes are in **`game.js`**, targeting the existing `<canvas id="game">`.
