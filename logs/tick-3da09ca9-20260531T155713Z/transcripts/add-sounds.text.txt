Implemented audio feedback:

* Created an `AudioContext` and helper `playSound(freq, duration)` to generate short square‑wave tones.
* Ensured the audio context resumes on first key press (`ensureAudioRunning`).
* Added a thrust sound (440 Hz) when the **Up** arrow is pressed.
* Added a collision sound (150 Hz) when the ship hits an asteroid.

These sounds are lightweight, generated on‑the‑fly, and enhance the game experience without requiring external audio files.
