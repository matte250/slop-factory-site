# Orbit Dodge  

**Concept**: A top‑down space game where the player pilots a small ship orbiting a planet. The ship can rotate around the planet and thrust outward to dodge incoming asteroids.  

**Core Mechanics**:  
- Left/right arrows rotate around the planet.  
- Up arrow thrusts away; down arrow brakes.  
- Asteroids spawn from the edges and move toward the planet.  

**Lose Condition**: Collision between any asteroid and the ship ends the game.  

**Why Canvas**: Uses simple shapes and real‑time animation, ideal for HTML canvas.
