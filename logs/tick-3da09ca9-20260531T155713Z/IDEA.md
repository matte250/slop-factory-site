# Orbit Dodge

**Concept**: A top‑down space game where the player pilots a small ship orbiting a planet. The ship can rotate around the planet and thrust outward to dodge incoming asteroids.

**Core Mechanics**:
- Use left/right arrows to rotate around the planet.
- Up arrow to thrust away from the planet, down arrow to brake.
- Asteroids spawn from the edges and move toward the planet.

**Lose Condition**: The game ends immediately when any asteroid collides with the ship.

**Why Canvas**: Simple shapes (circles, lines) and real‑time animation make it ideal for an HTML canvas implementation.
