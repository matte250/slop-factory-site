Implemented **game.js** with a minimal “Asteroid Rescue” game that:

- Targets the `<canvas id="game">` element.
- Handles ship movement via arrow keys.
- Spawns asteroids (lose condition) and astronauts (points).
- Drains fuel over time and ends the game on collision or fuel depletion.
- Renders ship, asteroids, astronauts, and UI (points & fuel).
