Implemented **game.js** with a concise endless‑runner built on the `<canvas id="game">` element.

Key features:
- Player square jumps with **Space/Up**.
- Procedurally generated platforms, occasional gaps, and obstacles.
- Simple gravity & collision detection; falling off or hitting an obstacle ends the game.
- Score displayed (distance‑based) and game‑over overlay.

The script runs automatically on page load and starts the game loop.
