# Game Idea: Astro Dodge
- Player controls a spaceship that moves horizontally along the bottom of an HTML canvas.  
- Asteroids fall from the top with increasing speed.  
- Optional power‑ups grant temporary shields.  
- **Lose condition:** The player loses if the ship collides with an asteroid or if an asteroid reaches the bottom of the canvas without being destroyed.
