# Canvas Game Idea: Asteroid Dodge

- Control a ship horizontally at the bottom of the canvas (mouse or arrow keys).
- Asteroids of varying size fall from the top, accelerating over time.
- Ship can fire a limited laser to destroy asteroids.
- **Lose condition**: Collision with an asteroid or any asteroid reaching the bottom.
- Simple graphics, collision detection, and scoring can be done with pure HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript.
