Implemented a concise **Pixel Dodger** game in `game.js` that:

- Targets the `<canvas id="game">` element.
- Features an auto‑moving square player that jumps with **Space**.
- Randomly spawns rectangular obstacles, with a chance for gaps.
- Handles gravity, collision detection, speed increase, score tracking, and a game‑over overlay.
- Allows click‑to‑restart after losing.
