# Game Idea: Asteroid Dodge

**Genre:** Endless runner (HTML canvas)

**Core Loop:** The player controls a spaceship that continuously moves forward. Asteroids spawn from the right side and drift left. The player can move up, down, left, and right to dodge them.

**Lose Condition:** The game ends when the ship collides with any asteroid.

**Win/Loss Metric:** Survive as long as possible; score = time survived.
