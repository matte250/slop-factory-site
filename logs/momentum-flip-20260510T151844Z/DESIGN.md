## Concept
The player pilots an ever‑accelerating dot, tapping to instantly flip its direction and weave through waves of spikes that spawn ahead of its current travel path.

## Player
The player controls a simple glowing dot. Verbs: flip direction (tap), maintain momentum (continuous), dodge (reactive movement).

## Core loop
Each moment the dot surges forward while the player watches incoming spikes appear along the forward line. The player decides the precise instant to tap and reverse velocity, timing flips to slip between spikes and stay alive. Successive flips become rhythmically tighter, rewarding quick perception and split‑second decision‑making.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Wall spikes** – static spikes that materialize on the wall directly ahead of the dot’s motion; they remain until the dot passes or flips away.
- **Moving sweepers** – long bars that slide down the wall in the direction of travel, sweeping across the path and forcing the player to flip before contact.
- **Burst emitters** – nodes that fire short bursts of spikes outward in a cone aligned with the current travel direction, creating temporary clusters to navigate.

## Score
- Flipping successfully without hitting a spike.
- Surviving a full wave of spikes (a set of obstacles that appears before a pause).
- Executing a chain of consecutive flips (combo) without collision.

## Win and lose
The player loses the moment the dot collides with any spike; the screen flashes and the dot shatters. The player wins by surviving a predefined number of waves or reaching a distant “exit portal” that appears after the final wave, accompanied by a bright burst and celebratory visual.

## Feel
A fast‑paced, reflex‑driven experience with a neon‑glow aesthetic against a dark, minimalist backdrop; the soundscape is tight chiptune synths and crisp arcade‑style beeps that punctuate each flip and collision.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of snapping direction at the exact instant a wall spikes materializes, turning a split‑second twitch into a satisfying dodge that feels like mastering momentum.
