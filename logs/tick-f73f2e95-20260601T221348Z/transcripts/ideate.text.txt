**IDEA.md** has been created with a concise HTML canvas game concept:

- **Title:** Nebula Escape  
- **Concept:** Top‑down space navigation; pilot a ship through an endless nebula with asteroids and hostile drones.  
- **Core Mechanics:** Continuous forward drift, arrow/WASD steering, collect energy orbs for shields/score.  
- **Lose Condition:** Ship destroyed when shield reaches zero after collisions (optional timer‑limit mode).  
- **Canvas Fit:** Simple 2D sprite rendering and collision detection, handling many moving objects efficiently.
