Implemented **`game.js`** with a concise, functional Cosmic Drift game:

- Targets the `<canvas id="game">` element.
- Handles mouse/touch input to steer a continuously‑moving ship.
- Spawns collectible particles and hazardous asteroids.
- Updates positions, detects collisions, tracks score, and ends on loss conditions.
