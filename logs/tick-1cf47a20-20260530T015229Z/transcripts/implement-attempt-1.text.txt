Implemented **`game.js`** with a concise Asteroid Dodge game:

- Targets the `<canvas id="game">` element.
- Handles player ship movement via arrow keys.
- Spawns and updates moving asteroids.
- Detects collisions (circle‑rect) and ends the game.
- Includes a 60‑second timer and simple HUD.
- Renders game loop with `requestAnimationFrame`.
