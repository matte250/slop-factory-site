Implemented sound effects:

- **Background music** plays in a loop (low volume) when the game starts.  
- **Collect sound** (`button‑16.mp3`) triggers each time a star is collected.  
- **Crash sound** (`button‑10.mp3`) plays once when the player collides or moves out of bounds, guarded by a `crashPlayed` flag to avoid repeated playback.  

Audio assets are loaded via `new Audio(...)` and integrated into the existing game loop, preserving current mechanics while adding an auditory layer.
