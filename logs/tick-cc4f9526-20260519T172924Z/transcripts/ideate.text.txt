# Game Idea: Space Dodger

**Genre**: Arcade / Endless runner  

**Core Loop**: The player pilots a small spaceship moving forward across a canvas. Asteroids of varying sizes spawn from the right and drift left. The ship can move up/down (or in 2‑D) to avoid collisions.

**Controls**: Arrow keys or WASD.

**Win Condition**: Accumulate distance (or points) indefinitely.

**Lose Condition**: Collision with any asteroid ends the game and shows the final score.

**Visuals**: Simple shapes (triangles for the ship, circles for asteroids) drawn with the Canvas API.

**Extensions**: Power‑ups (shield, speed boost), increasing asteroid speed over time, high‑score leaderboard.
