# Game Idea: Space Dodger

**Genre**: Arcade / Endless runner

**Core Loop**: The player controls a small spaceship that continuously moves forward across an HTML canvas. Asteroids of varying sizes spawn from the right and drift left. The ship can move up/down (or in 2‑D) to avoid collisions.

**Controls**: Arrow keys or WASD to move the ship.

**Win Condition**: Accumulate distance (or points) for as long as possible.

**Lose Condition**: The ship collides with any asteroid – the game ends and the final score is shown.

**Visuals**: Simple shapes (triangles for the ship, circles for asteroids) rendered with Canvas API, making it lightweight and easy to implement.

**Extensions**: Power‑ups (shield, speed boost), increasing asteroid speed over time, high‑score leaderboard.
