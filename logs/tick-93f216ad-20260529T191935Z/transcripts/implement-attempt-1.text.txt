Implemented **game.js** with a minimal “Space Debris Dodger”:

- Targets the `<canvas id="game">` element.
- Handles ship movement (←/→) and shooting (Space).
- Spawns falling debris that speeds up as health drops.
- Detects collisions, tracks health (3 lives), and ends the game with a “Game Over” message.
- Draws ship, bullets, debris, and health counter each frame.
