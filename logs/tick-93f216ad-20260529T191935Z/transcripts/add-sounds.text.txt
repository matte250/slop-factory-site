Added audio feedback:

* Initialized an `AudioContext` with helper `playTone`.
* Defined sound functions:
  * `soundLaser` – short laser tone on shooting.
  * `soundExplosion` – noise burst on debris destruction.
  * `soundHit` – lower tone when the ship is hit.
  * `soundGameOver` – descending tone on game over.
* Triggered `audioCtx.resume()` on any key press to unlock audio on browsers.
* Played appropriate sounds in the main loop:
  * Laser sound when firing.
  * Hit sound on ship‑debris collision.
  * Explosion sound on bullet‑debris collision.
  * Game‑over sound when health reaches zero.

All changes are within `game.js`; the gameplay remains unchanged while now providing richer audio cues.
