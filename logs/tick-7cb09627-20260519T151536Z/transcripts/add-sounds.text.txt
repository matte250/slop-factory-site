Added sound effects:

- **Audio context** creation with helper `playTone`.
- Functions for **collision**, **score**, and **game‑over** sounds.
- Unlock audio on the first click or key press.
- Play collision tone when a block hits the player.
- Play score tone each time a block passes the bottom.
- Play game‑over tone when the player loses.

All sound code is integrated into `game.js`; just keep the existing `<script src="game.js"></script>` reference. The game now has richer graphics and audio feedback.
