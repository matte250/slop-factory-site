Implemented audio features:

- Created an `AudioContext` and `playTone` helper for simple sound effects.  
- Added a call to `audioCtx.resume()` on the first key press to unlock audio playback.  
- Played a low‑frequency tone when the ship collides with an asteroid (game over).  

These changes integrate basic sound feedback into the Asteroid Miner game while preserving the existing graphics enhancements.
