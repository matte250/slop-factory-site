# Random Canvas Game Idea

**Title:** Asteroid Dodge

**Concept:** The player controls a small spaceship that can move left and right along the bottom of the canvas. Asteroids of varying sizes and speeds spawn at random positions at the top and fall toward the bottom.

**Goal:** Survive as long as possible by dodging the falling asteroids.

**Lose Condition:** The game ends immediately when any asteroid collides with the spaceship.

**Optional Hook:** The longer the player survives, the faster the asteroids become, and the score increments with each avoided asteroid.
