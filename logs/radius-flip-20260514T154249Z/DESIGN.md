## Concept
The player guides an endlessly forward‑moving dot that can toggle between a minuscule point to slip through tight gaps and a massive sphere to smash obstacles, creating a rhythmic tension‑and‑release loop.

## Player
The avatar is a glowing dot that expands and contracts. Its only verb is **toggle size** (tap or press) which switches it between tiny and large forms; the dot always auto‑advances forward.

## Core loop
Every few seconds a new obstacle or passage appears. The player watches the upcoming geometry, decides whether to stay in the default size, shrink to thread a narrow gap, or enlarge to crush a barrier, then taps to toggle size. The cooldown after a large‑size smash forces a brief period of careful navigation before the next decision, keeping the forward momentum relentless.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Narrow gaps**: only traversable when the dot is in its tiny form.
- **Solid blocks**: block the path unless destroyed while the dot is large.
- **Moving sweepers**: horizontal or vertical walls that slide across the lane, requiring timing to avoid.
- **Spikes**: stationary lethal points that kill on contact with any size.
- **Overhead arches**: low ceilings that force a tiny form, otherwise cause a collision.

## Score
- **Survived obstacle**: each gap or barrier passed without collision.
- **Destroyed block**: each obstacle crushed while in large form.
- **Combo streak**: consecutive successful toggles without taking damage, rewarding skillful timing.

## Win and lose
- **Win**: Reach the end of the level or distance goal, triggering a celebratory burst of light and a final score tally.
- **Lose**: Collide with any obstacle while in the wrong size, causing the dot to explode and a game‑over screen displaying the final score.

## Feel
The game feels frantic yet elegant, with a steady forward thrust punctuated by sudden bursts of power. A neon‑glow aesthetic against a dark backdrop gives a retro‑futuristic vibe, while a pulsing synth‑chiptune soundtrack underscores each size change with satisfying beats and a crunchy smash sound when obstacles are destroyed.

## Why it's fun
The split‑second decision to shrink through a tiny opening or grow to obliterate a looming barrier delivers an instant, visceral “aha!” that makes players grin, curse, and keep trying for that perfect timing.
