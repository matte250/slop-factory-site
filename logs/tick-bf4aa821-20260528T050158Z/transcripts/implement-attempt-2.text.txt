Implemented **Orbital Dodge** game in `game.js`. The script targets the canvas with `id="game"` and provides:

- Spacecraft orbit controls (arrow keys)
- Asteroid spawning and movement toward the planet
- Collision detection for ship‑asteroid and ship‑planet
- Simple rendering of planet, ship, and asteroids
- Score display (elapsed time) and game‑over overlay

Add this `game.js` to your HTML page (e.g., `<script src="game.js"></script>`) and ensure a `<canvas id="game"></canvas>` element exists. Enjoy the game!
