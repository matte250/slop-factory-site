Implemented audio feedback:

- Added `AudioContext` with `playTone(freq, duration)` helper.
- **Jump** (Space) plays a 440 Hz tone; **slide** (Down Arrow) plays a 220 Hz tone.
- Game‑over triggers a low 110 Hz tone.
- Integrated sound calls into the existing keydown handler and loss condition.

Now the game provides simple sound effects for player actions and the end of a run. Add `<script src="game.js"></script>` as before to activate the audio.
