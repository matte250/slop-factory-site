Implemented audio features:

- Created an `AudioContext` and `playTone` helper for simple sine‑wave tones.
- Resumes the audio context on the first key press.
- Plays a short tone when an asteroid spawns.
- Plays a higher‑pitch tone each time the player dodges an asteroid (score increment).
- Plays a lower‑pitch, longer tone on collision/game over.

The game now includes background starfield graphics and sound effects for spawning, scoring, and collisions. Load `game.js` in your HTML to experience the enhanced version.
