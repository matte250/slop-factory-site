# Game Idea: Asteroid Dodge

- Player controls a ship at the bottom of the canvas.  
- Asteroids spawn at the top and fall, accelerating over time.  
- Move left/right with arrow keys to dodge.  
- **Lose condition:** an asteroid collides with the ship.  
- Score is based on time survived or asteroids avoided.
