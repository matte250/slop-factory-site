**IDEA.md** has been created with a concise game concept:

**Canvas Asteroid Dodge**  
- Top‑down spaceship controlled with arrow keys (rotate & thrust)  
- Endless stream of moving asteroids  
- Colliding with an asteroid ends the game (lose condition)  
- Optional power‑ups (shield, speed boost)  
- Simple vector graphics (circles, triangles) drawn on an HTML5 canvas
