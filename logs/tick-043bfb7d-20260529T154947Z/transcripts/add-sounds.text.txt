Added sound support:

* **Background music** (`bg.mp3`) loops at low volume.
* **Jump sound** (`jump.wav`) plays each time the player jumps.
* **Death sound** (`die.wav`) plays on collision or falling, and background music stops.

Audio objects are created at the start of the script, playback is safely wrapped in `.catch()` to avoid autoplay blocks, and the sounds are triggered in the appropriate game events.
