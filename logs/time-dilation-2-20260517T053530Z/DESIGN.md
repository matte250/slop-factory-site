## Concept
The player pilots a tiny dot through an endless tunnel, using a tap‑activated bubble that briefly slows the world while the dot keeps its regular speed, creating a fleeting window to navigate tight obstacles. The bubble’s cooldown forces careful timing and strategic use.

## Player
The player controls a glowing dot that automatically moves forward through a scrolling tunnel. The player can steer left and right and tap to spawn a short‑lived time‑dilation bubble. The dot’s motion remains at normal speed at all times.

## Core loop
Every few seconds a new segment of the tunnel appears, populated with obstacles that rush toward the dot. The player watches the incoming pattern, decides whether to dodge by steering or to activate the bubble, and then reacts within the brief slow‑motion window. After the bubble expires a cooldown period begins, during which it cannot be used again, and the tunnel returns to full speed.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Sweeping walls** that slide horizontally across the tunnel, narrowing the passage.
- **Spinning blades** that rotate around a fixed point, sweeping a small arc.
- **Pulsing barriers** that expand and contract, momentarily blocking the path.
- **Static spikes** that jut from the tunnel floor or ceiling, requiring precise steering.
All hazards move consistently with the tunnel’s scrolling speed, disappearing once they pass the player.

## Score
Score increases each time the player successfully passes an obstacle segment. Bonus points are awarded for navigating a segment without using the bubble, rewarding skillful timing. Collectible orbs that appear occasionally grant extra points when grabbed.

## Win and lose
The player loses instantly on collision with any hazard, triggering a Game Over overlay. The player wins by surviving a predefined sequence of obstacle segments (e.g., completing a full tunnel cycle) or reaching a time threshold, after which a celebratory animation and score summary appear.

## Feel
Frantic yet rhythmic, with fast‑paced scrolling interrupted by brief pockets of slow motion that feel like a cinematic bullet‑time. Neon‑lit tunnel walls and glowing obstacles contrast against a dark background, accompanied by a pulsing synth soundtrack that accents the bubble activation.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of stretching a split‑second reaction into a slow‑motion window, letting the player dramatically dodge danger and feel mastery over time.
