## Concept
The player guides a constantly advancing dot that is pulled toward a gravity anchor on either side; tapping flips the anchor, curving the dot’s path to weave through a series of horizontally offset gaps.

## Player
The avatar is a small glowing dot. Its only verb is to *toggle* the gravity anchor left or right (a tap or click). This single action determines the curvature of its trajectory.

## Core loop
The dot moves forward automatically. The player watches upcoming obstacles and decides, on each beat, which side to pull the dot toward. Switching the anchor gently steers the dot into the next gap. After the dot passes a gap, a new offset gap appears, and the decision‑making rhythm repeats, keeping the player engaged in a continuous flow of anticipation and reaction.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Offset Gap Walls**: Solid vertical barriers with a single horizontal opening that the dot must align with. The opening’s position shifts left or right each time.
- **Moving Sweepers**: Horizontal bars that glide across the playfield, briefly blocking portions of a gap and demanding precise timing.
- **Closing Spikes**: Short spikes that emerge from the walls when the dot lingers too long, encouraging forward momentum.
Each hazard threatens collision and forces the player to plan anchor switches ahead of time.

## Score
Score increases each time the dot successfully passes through a gap. Bonus points are awarded for chaining consecutive flawless passages without a miss.

## Win and lose
The player loses instantly upon colliding with any solid part of an obstacle; a “Game Over” overlay appears with the final score and restart instructions. The player wins by navigating a predefined sequence of gaps (e.g., completing the final gap of a level) without collision, triggering a celebratory “You Win!” overlay.

## Feel
A rhythmic, flowing experience with a neon‑glow aesthetic against a dark backdrop. The pace feels steady yet increasingly tense as gaps shift, and subtle synthetic tones punctuate each successful maneuver and collision.

## Why it's fun
Each tap instantly reshapes the dot’s path, turning simple timing into a satisfying dance of weaving through tight gaps.
