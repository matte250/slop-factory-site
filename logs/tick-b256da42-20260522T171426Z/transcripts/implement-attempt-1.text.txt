Implemented **Nebula Runner** as `game.js`:

- Targets the `<canvas id="game">` element.
- Handles resizing, ship movement (arrow keys), random asteroid spawning, collision detection, scoring, and game‑over state.
- Renders ship, asteroids, background, and UI with simple AABB collision logic.

Add `<script src="game.js"></script>` to your HTML to run the game.
