Added sound effects to **Nebula Runner**:

* Initialized audio objects (collision, game‑over, point) with tiny embedded WAV data URIs.  
* Played the *collision* and *game‑over* sounds when the ship hits an asteroid.  
* Played a *point* sound each time an asteroid leaves the screen and the score increments.  

All other gameplay and graphics remain unchanged; just include the same `<script src="game.js"></script>` tag to enable the sounds.
