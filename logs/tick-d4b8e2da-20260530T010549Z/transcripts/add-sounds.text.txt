Implemented audio:

* Background music (`bgm.mp3`) loops softly.  
* Thrust sound plays while the ship is moving, stopping when idle.  
* Explosion sound triggers once on collision, pauses music, and prevents repeat playback.

Added a `explosionPlayed` flag and integrated sound control into the update loop. The game now has richer visual effects and sound feedback while still targeting the existing `<canvas id="game">`.
