Implemented audio for the asteroid‑shower game:

* **Audio setup** – created an `AudioContext`, tone generator (`playTone`) and three helper functions: `playLaser`, `playExplosion`, `playGameOver`.
* **Laser firing** – now triggers a short laser tone.
* **Collisions** – asteroid‑ship hits, bottom hits, and asteroid destruction each play appropriate sounds (game‑over tone or explosion tone).

All changes are in `game.js`; the game now includes sound feedback while preserving existing mechanics and graphics.
