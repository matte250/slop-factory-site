## Concept
The player taps to toggle a dot between a tiny and a large form, using the tiny shape to slip through narrow gaps and the large shape to crush small obstacles. Timing the size change creates a rhythmic puzzle of fitting and smashing.

## Player
A single circular dot that travels forward through a scrolling tunnel. Visually it is a simple solid shape that can appear tiny or enlarged. Verb: toggle size (tap/click). The dot’s position is controlled automatically by the level flow; the player does not move it directly.

## Core loop
The game continuously scrolls forward, presenting a sequence of gaps and obstacles. Every few seconds a new challenge appears, and the player must decide whether to stay tiny to thread a narrow opening or grow large to smash a blocking block. The player reads the upcoming layout, taps to switch size, and watches the result unfold, repeating the decision rhythmically as the tunnel advances.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Narrow passages**: walls that leave only a slim corridor; the dot must be in its tiny form to pass safely.
- **Crushable blocks**: solid obstacles that block the path but are destroyed when the dot is large, clearing the way.
- **Static spikes**: deadly spikes that kill the dot regardless of size if collided with.
- **Moving sweepers**: horizontal bars that slide across the tunnel, requiring a timely size change to avoid contact.
- **Falling shards**: small pieces that descend; they are harmless to a large dot but can snag a tiny dot.

## Score
Score increases when the player:
- Successfully passes through a narrow passage.
- Crushes a block with the large form.
- Survives an entire wave of obstacles without dying.
Points are awarded per event, encouraging both precision and aggression.

## Win and lose
**Lose**: colliding with any obstacle while in an inappropriate size (e.g., hitting a narrow wall while large, or touching spikes in any form). On death the screen shows “Game Over”, the final score, and restart instructions.
**Win**: completing the final set of waves (or reaching a predefined score threshold) triggers a celebratory overlay with “You Win!” and the final tally.

## Feel
The game feels fast‑paced yet contemplative: a steady forward flow punctuated by quick, decisive size toggles. A neon‑lit aesthetic with high‑contrast cyan and magenta accents gives a retro‑futuristic vibe, while crisp chiptune blips punctuate each successful toggle, crush, or passage. The rhythm of toggling creates a satisfying loop of anticipation and payoff.

## Why it's fun
The moment you nail a perfect size switch—slipping through an impossible gap or pulverizing an obstacle in a single tap—delivers a burst of triumph that makes you grin, curse, and keep trying for the next perfect rhythm.
