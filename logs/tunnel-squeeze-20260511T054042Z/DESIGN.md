## Concept
The player taps to briefly compress the tunnel’s width, expanding the safe gap around the moving dot just enough to thread through tight sections before the tunnel returns to its normal size.

## Player
A glowing dot that automatically advances forward through an endless tunnel. The player’s only verb is **squeeze** – a tap or click that triggers a short‑duration tunnel compression.

## Core loop
1. The dot continuously scrolls forward while the tunnel scrolls toward the player.
2. Obstacles appear as static or moving protrusions that leave irregular gaps.
3. The player watches upcoming gaps and decides whether to **squeeze**.
4. If a squeeze is timed, the tunnel contracts, temporarily widening the selected gap and allowing the dot to pass.
5. The tunnel expands again, and the next segment arrives. This cycle repeats every few seconds.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Solid walls** on the left and right edges that end the run on contact.
- **Spike clusters** that project from either wall, creating narrow passages.
- **Rotating blades** that sweep across the tunnel’s center, requiring precise timing.
- **Moving blocks** that slide horizontally, altering the shape of the gap while the tunnel is normal.
All hazards are positioned so that the dot can survive without input for at least the first three seconds, but they become increasingly dense.

## Score
- **Gap passage** – each time the dot successfully threads a tight gap while a squeeze is active.
- **Orbs** – occasional glowing pickups that appear in safe zones; collecting one adds points.
- **Combo** – consecutive successful squeezes without a miss increase the per‑gap score.

## Win and lose
- **Lose**: the dot touches any wall, spike, blade, or moving block.
- **Win**: reaching a predefined distance (e.g., the tunnel’s “exit” after a set number of segments) or surviving a long time threshold. When the game ends, a “Game Over” or “You Win!” overlay appears with the final score and restart instructions.

## Feel
The game feels frantic yet rhythmic: a pulsing neon tunnel that contracts and expands in sync with the player’s tap, set against a dark backdrop with sharp electronic beeps. Visuals are high‑contrast neon line art, and sound cues emphasize each successful squeeze and each collision.

## Why it's fun
The single, timed squeeze that instantly turns a near‑miss into a clean pass creates a punchy moment of triumph that makes players grin, gasp, and keep tapping for the next perfect timing.
