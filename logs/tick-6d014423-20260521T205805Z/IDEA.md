# Game Idea: Pixel Runner

A minimalist side‑scrolling endless runner where the player controls a small pixel square that can jump to avoid incoming obstacles (spikes, gaps) and collect points. The canvas renders a scrolling world with simple shapes and a moving background. **Lose condition:** The player loses when the pixel collides with an obstacle or falls off the bottom of the canvas. The goal is to survive as long as possible and achieve a high score.
