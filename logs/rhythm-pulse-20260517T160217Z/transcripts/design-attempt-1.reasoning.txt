## Concept
Rapid reflex rhythm game where a continuously moving dot fires pulsing waves on player taps; timing the pulses to the beat pushes obstacles aside, creating momentary safe corridors.

## Player

## Core loop
Each beat (roughly 1–2 seconds) a visual/audio cue appears; obstacles continuously spawn ahead and move toward the dot. ## Hazards / Obstacles

- Pulseable blocks: regular obstacles that are pushed aside by the pulse for a short window before returning.

- +1 point for each obstacle successfully cleared (i.e., avoided via pulse or by passing untouched).
