# Game Idea: "Orbital Escape"

- **Genre:** Minimalist arcade
- **Core Loop:** The player controls a small satellite orbiting a planet, using the mouse or arrow keys to adjust its speed and trajectory.
- **Objective:** Collect floating energy orbs that appear randomly while maintaining a stable orbit.
- **Lose Condition:** The satellite crashes into the planet or flies off-screen (exits the orbital area).
- **Canvas Implementation:** Simple physics for circular motion, drawing the planet, satellite, and orbs with HTML5 canvas.
- **Replayability:** Increasing orbit speed and more frequent orbs as the player scores higher.
