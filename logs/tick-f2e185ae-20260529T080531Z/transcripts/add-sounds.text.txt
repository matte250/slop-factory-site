Added full audio support:

- Created an `AudioContext` with helper functions `playBeep` (for radius toggle) and `playExplosion` (on collision).
- Ensured the audio context resumes on user interaction.
- Integrated a beep sound when the ship changes orbit radius.
- Played an explosion noise when an asteroid hits the planet, triggering game over.

All sound effects are generated procedurally, requiring no external assets.
