## Concept
The player taps to split an ever‑expanding pulse ring into two concentric waves, threading them through nested obstacles and re‑uniting them for a satisfying score boost.

## Player
The player controls a simple tap cursor that initiates a split of the current pulse. No movement; the only verb is **tap** to split, and optionally **hold** to time the recombination.

## Core loop
Every few seconds a pulse expands outward. The player watches the ring approach obstacles, decides the optimal moment to tap, splits the pulse, guides the two inner rings through gaps, and then times their recombination for extra points. Successive splits keep the rhythm flowing and raise tension.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Nested barriers**: stationary concentric rings with apertures that only one of the two split pulses can pass through at a time.
- **Rotating arcs**: arcs that sweep around the center, temporarily blocking a path and forcing the player to choose a different split timing.
- **Pulsing walls**: walls that expand and contract, creating windows of opportunity that must be timed precisely.
- **Dynamic spikes**: short‑lived spikes that shoot outward from the edge; they disappear after a beat, encouraging quick splits.

## Score
- **Recombine**: when the two split pulses merge back together after successfully navigating obstacles.
- **Perfect timing**: tapping exactly as the pulse touches an aperture grants a bonus multiplier.
- **Chain**: consecutive successful recombinations without a miss increase a combo counter that adds extra points.

## Win and lose
- **Win**: reach a predefined number of successful recombinations or survive a set number of pulse cycles; the screen flashes and the pulse steadies into a luminous final bloom.
- **Lose**: any split pulse collides with a solid obstacle or fails to recombine before the pulse dissipates; the ring fractures and fades, accompanied by a brief ripple of darkness.

## Feel
A steady, hypnotic rhythm punctuated by sharp, tactile moments of split and recombine. Visuals favor a calm neon glow against a dark backdrop, with smooth gradients and subtle particle trails. Audio consists of soft ambient drones punctuated by clear, resonant chimes on each split and a satisfying crescendo when pulses merge.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of slicing a perfect expanding ring, threading the halves through tight gaps, and hearing the satisfying click as they reunite.
