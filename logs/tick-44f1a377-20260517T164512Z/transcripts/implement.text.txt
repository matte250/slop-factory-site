Implemented **canvas‑based endless runner** per the IDEA.md specification.

Key features:
- Auto‑moving circle (player) with space‑bar / tap jump.
- Procedurally generated platforms, obstacles, and collectible stars.
- Simple physics (gravity, jump, ground detection) and collision handling.
- Score based on distance and collected stars, with a Game‑Over overlay.
- Responsive canvas resizing using device pixel ratio.

The new `game.js` targets the first `<canvas>` element on the page and runs automatically when loaded.
