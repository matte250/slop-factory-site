# Random HTML Canvas Game Idea

**Title:** Shadow Sprint

**Concept:**
- The player controls a small square that constantly moves forward across an endless scrolling canvas.
- Randomly generated "shadow" obstacles appear from the right and move left.
- The player can tap or press a key to briefly jump over obstacles.

**Lose Condition:**
- The game ends when the player's square collides with a shadow obstacle.

**Goal:**
- Survive as long as possible, with the score increasing over time.
