Added sound effects using the Web Audio API:

* **Jump sound** – a short 440 Hz tone played each time the player jumps.  
* **Game‑over sound** – a low 150 Hz tone triggered when a collision ends the game.  
* Audio context is resumed on the first user interaction to meet browser autoplay policies.

The rest of the game logic and graphics remain unchanged. Include `game.js` in the page as before; the canvas will now play the new sounds.
