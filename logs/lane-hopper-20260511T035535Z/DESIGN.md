## Concept
The player guides a constantly advancing dot through a three‑lane tunnel, tapping to shift the dot up one lane (wrapping around) and dodge lane‑specific obstacles. The satisfaction comes from the rhythmic flow of anticipating patterns and executing split‑second lane changes.

## Player
The avatar is a simple glowing dot that moves forward automatically. The player can tap or press a key to shift the dot up one lane, wrapping to the bottom lane when necessary. **Verb:** shift lane.

## Core loop
Obstacles appear ahead in each lane as the dot moves forward. The player watches the upcoming pattern, decides whether to stay or shift, and taps exactly when a lane becomes safe. Every few moments a new set of obstacles arrives, demanding continuous attention and timing.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Stationary blocks** that occupy a lane for a short distance, forcing the player to be in a different lane.
- **Sweeping barriers** that slide horizontally within a lane, opening and closing gaps.
- **Pulsing lasers** that flash on and off, creating timing windows.
- **Spike traps** that pop up briefly, requiring a lane change to avoid.
- **Full‑lane walls** that briefly close an entire lane, making the timing of a lane shift critical.

## Score
- Each obstacle the player passes without collision awards points.
- Perfectly timed lane changes that occur just before a hazard clears grant a bonus.
- Collectible floating orbs that appear in safe lanes add extra points.

## Win and lose
- **Lose:** Colliding with any obstacle triggers a game‑over state showing the final score and a restart prompt.
- **Win:** Surviving the final wave of obstacles (the tunnel’s end) celebrates the player with a burst of visual and audio feedback.

## Feel
The game feels fast‑paced and rhythmic, bathed in neon‑glow visuals that emphasize the three‑lane tunnel. A synth‑driven soundtrack provides a steady pulse, with sharp beeps for lane changes and a crisp crash for collisions. The overall vibe is frantic yet precise, encouraging players to find a flow state.

## Why it's fun
Nailing a split‑second lane swap just as a wall rushes past delivers an instant, satisfying “clear” moment that makes the player grin or shout.
