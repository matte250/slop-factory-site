## Concept
The player guides a glowing dot and can tap to rewind time for a short distance, pulling the dot and all on‑screen obstacles back to undo a recent mistake. A cooldown after each rewind forces strategic timing.

## Player
A single luminous dot that the player moves around the play area.  Verbs: move (steer) and rewind (tap to trigger time reversal).

## Core loop
Every few seconds new obstacles appear and travel across the screen while the dot constantly drifts forward. The player continuously steers the dot to avoid collisions, deciding in the moment whether to use a rewind to undo an imminent hit. After a rewind the ability is unavailable for a short cooldown, after which the player can again choose to rewind or keep dodging.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- Linear sweepers: fast bars that traverse the screen horizontally or vertically, telegraphing their path before crossing.
- Homing blips: small particles that lock onto the dot’s current position and chase it for a brief interval.
- Static spikes: stationary shapes that appear in clusters and remain lethal until they fade.
- Expanding rings: circles that grow outward from a point, forcing the player to stay outside the expanding perimeter.

## Score
- Avoiding an obstacle for a set interval adds points.
- Successfully rewinding to evade a collision grants a bonus.
- Collecting fleeting time‑crystals that appear after a rewind awards additional score.

## Win and lose
The player loses when the dot collides with any obstacle, triggering a Game Over overlay with final score and restart instructions. The player wins by reaching a target score threshold (e.g., achieving a high‑score milestone) or surviving a predefined duration, after which a Victory screen celebrates the achievement.

## Feel
Frantic and tight‑rope‑walking gameplay with moments of calm when the rewind is available, wrapped in a neon‑glow aesthetic and a pulsating chiptune soundtrack that accentuates each action.

## Why it's fun
The instant gratification of pulling a perfect rewind to erase a mistake creates a delicious loop of tension and relief.
