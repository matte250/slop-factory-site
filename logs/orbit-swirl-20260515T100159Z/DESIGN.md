## Concept
In Orbit Swirl the player controls a glowing dot that continuously orbits a static central point. Tapping instantly flips the orbital direction, allowing the player to weave through incoming obstacles that travel along fixed radial lanes.

## Player
The player controls a single orbiting dot, visually a bright neon sphere.
Verbs: orbit (automatic), reverse (tap/click) to change orbital direction.

## Core loop
The dot circles the center at a steady pace. Obstacles appear in radial lanes and move outward (or inward) toward the orbit. The player watches the approaching hazards and chooses the right moment to tap, flipping the orbit direction to slip between lanes. Each decision feels immediate as the dot’s path instantly reverses, creating a rhythm of anticipation and reaction.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Radial lanes**: fixed angular sectors (e.g., left/right) that constantly emit moving barriers. Barriers travel along the lane toward the orbit and disappear after passing.
- **Pulsing blocks**: larger obstacles that expand briefly within a lane, forcing the player to time a reversal to avoid collision.
- **Rotating spikes**: segments that rotate around the center at a different radius; they act as moving gaps the dot must thread through, creating timing puzzles.

## Score
- Each obstacle successfully avoided awards a point.
- Passing a complete radial lane without collision grants a bonus streak score.
- Reversing direction while an obstacle is near the dot gives a “perfect dodge” multiplier.

## Win and lose
The player loses when the orbiting dot collides with any obstacle, triggering a “Game Over” overlay. The player wins by reaching a predefined high‑score target or surviving a set number of obstacle waves, after which a celebratory “You Win!” screen appears.

## Feel
Fast‑paced and reflexive, with a neon‑glow aesthetic against a dark backdrop. Visuals pulse with each reversal, and subtle synth tones accompany actions, creating an arcade‑style, kinetic atmosphere.

## Why it's fun
The instant reversal mechanic gives a satisfying “flip‑the‑orbit” moment that lets players feel in‑the‑instant control over a chaotic stream of hazards.
