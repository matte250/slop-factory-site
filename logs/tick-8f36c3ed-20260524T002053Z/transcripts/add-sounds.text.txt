Implemented full audio support:

* Added `AudioContext` setup and helper functions for thrust, explosion, collect, and game‑over tones.
* Integrated sound triggers:
  * Thrust sound during acceleration.
  * Explosion sound on asteroid collision.
  * Collect sound when picking up energy orbs.
  * Game‑over sound when fuel runs out or the ship is destroyed (played once).
* Resumed suspended audio context on the first key press to satisfy browser autoplay policies.
* Updated game loop to ensure the game‑over sound plays only once.
