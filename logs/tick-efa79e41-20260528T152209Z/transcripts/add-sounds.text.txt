Added audio feedback:

* Created an `AudioContext` and a helper `playTone(freq, duration)` that plays short sine‑wave tones.
* Player movement (left/right) now triggers a subtle tone (200 Hz, 0.05 s).
* Scoring a circle plays a higher tone (400 Hz, 0.08 s).
* Collision/game‑over plays a low, longer tone (100 Hz, 0.5 s).

All other graphics enhancements remain unchanged, and the game continues to work with the `<canvas id="game">` element. The sounds are generated in‑browser without external assets.
