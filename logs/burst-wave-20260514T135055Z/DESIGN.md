## Concept
The player controls a wandering dot that can emit a brief, expanding pulse to instantly vaporize any obstacles directly ahead, then must wait for a cooldown before pulsing again. The tension of timing each pulse against an ever‑approaching threat creates a satisfying loop of risk and reward.

## Player
A small, constantly moving glowing dot serves as the avatar. The player can:
- **Move** freely in any direction within the play area.
- **Pulse** – a single tap (or click) launches an outward shockwave that clears obstacles in a short forward radius.
- **Wait** – strategically keep distance while the pulse recharges.

## Core loop
1. Obstacles continuously spawn and drift toward the player.
2. The player steers to stay in safe zones and watches the cooldown timer.
3. When the pulse is ready, the player chooses the optimal moment to trigger it, clearing a swath of hazards.
4. The cooldown begins, and the cycle repeats, with obstacle speed and density gradually increasing.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Rolling shards**: fast, linear projectiles that sweep across the screen; they can be destroyed if caught in the pulse’s forward arc.
- **Static mines**: stationary spikes that detonate when the player touches them; the pulse disarms them in advance.
- **Bursting orbs**: objects that linger briefly before exploding outward; the pulse can neutralize them before they erupt.
- **Chasing shadows**: entities that home toward the player’s current location; they are only vulnerable while within the pulse’s forward cone.

## Score
Score increases on discrete events such as:
- Each obstacle destroyed by a pulse.
- Surviving a wave of obstacles without taking damage.
- Collecting occasional power‑ups that appear after a successful pulse.
A high score is displayed continuously during play.

## Win and lose
- **Lose**: the player collides with any active obstacle while the pulse is unavailable or the obstacle is outside the pulse’s radius.
- **Win**: the player reaches a predefined score threshold (or survives a fixed duration) that signifies mastery of the pulse mechanic. Upon win or loss, the canvas overlays a clear message (“Game Over” or “You Win!”) along with the final score and restart instructions.

## Feel
The game feels frantic yet rhythmic, with each pulse creating a bright neon flash that punctuates the constant onslaught of dark, kinetic hazards. A minimal synth soundtrack underscores the action, and visual effects use high‑contrast neon accents against a dark backdrop, delivering a pulse‑driven arcade experience.

## Why it's fun
Timing a single, powerful burst to clear an imminent swarm gives a rush of empowerment and makes every successful pulse feel like a triumphant strike.
