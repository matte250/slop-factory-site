# Game Idea: Celestial Runner

**Genre:** Endless runner on an HTML canvas.

**Core Loop:** The player controls a rocket that automatically moves forward across a star‑filled space background. Tap or click to thrust upward; gravity pulls the rocket down.

**Obstacles:** Randomly generated asteroids and laser barriers scroll toward the rocket. The player must dodge them.

**Lose Condition:** The game ends when the rocket collides with any obstacle or flies off the top/bottom edge of the canvas.

**Goal:** Survive as long as possible, accumulating points based on distance traveled.
