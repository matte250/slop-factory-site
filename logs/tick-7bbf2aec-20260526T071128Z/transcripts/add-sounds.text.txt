Implemented sound effects:

* Added an `AudioContext` and helper `playTone` to generate simple square‑wave tones.
* Defined specific sounds:
  * **Jump** – 440 Hz short tone.
  * **Collect** – 880 Hz short tone.
  * **Game Over** – 110 Hz longer tone.
* Integrated audio:
  * Resumes the audio context on the first user interaction.
  * Plays the jump sound when the player jumps.
  * Plays the collect sound when a dot is gathered.
  * Plays the game‑over sound on collision with an obstacle.

The game now features audible feedback for key actions while preserving existing gameplay and graphics.
