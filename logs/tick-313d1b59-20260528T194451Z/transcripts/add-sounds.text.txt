Added basic sound effects:

* **Audio setup** – creates an `AudioContext` (resumed on the first key press).  
* **playTone()** – helper that plays a short oscillator tone.  
* **Collision** – plays a low‑frequency square wave when the ship hits an asteroid (game over).  
* **Fuel collection** – plays a higher‑frequency triangle wave when a fuel cell is collected.  

The audio is lightweight, uses the Web Audio API, and integrates seamlessly with the existing game loop.
