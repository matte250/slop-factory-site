Implemented sound effects:

- Added an `AudioContext` with a helper `playSound(freq, duration)` that creates a short sine‑wave tone.
- Played a tone when the ship collects debris (`score++`).
- Played a lower tone when the ship collides with an asteroid (game over).

These audio cues are triggered directly in the collision logic, enhancing the game’s feedback while preserving the existing gameplay and graphics.
