Implemented **game.js** with a concise side‑scrolling game:

- Targets the canvas element with id `game`.
- Handles ship movement via arrow keys.
- Spawns debris to collect (increase score) and asteroids that end the game on collision.
- Includes a countdown timer; game ends when time runs out or an asteroid hits.
- Renders ship, debris, asteroids, and HUD (score & timer) and shows a “Game Over” overlay.
