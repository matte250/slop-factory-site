Implemented a concise **Astro Dodge** game in `game.js`:

- Targets the canvas with id `game`.
- Handles ship movement, fuel, and input (arrow keys/WASD).
- Spawns and updates drifting asteroids.
- Detects collisions and fuel depletion for game‑over.
- Renders ship, asteroids, fuel HUD, and “Game Over” message.
