Gravity Flip
=============

A minimalist HTML canvas game where the player controls a small ball that bounces on rotating platforms. Tap or click to flip gravity, causing the ball to stick to the ceiling instead of the floor. The platform rotation and gravity flips create a rhythm‑based challenge.

**Lose condition:** The player loses if the ball falls off the bottom or top edge of the canvas (i.e., leaves the visible area).
