Implemented richer graphics:

* **Starfield background** – 100 twinkling stars that drift downward, giving depth.
* **Ship rendering** – gradient hull and an orange thrust flame that appears when the up‑arrow is pressed.
* **Asteroid rendering** – each asteroid now has a random rotation, rotation speed, and a radial gradient for a rocky look.
* **Update loop** – advances the starfield and sets `ship.thrust` based on the up‑arrow key.

All changes are in `game.js`; no other files were modified. The game now has a more polished visual style while preserving the original mechanics.
