# Game Idea: Shadow Dash

A side‑scrolling endless runner on an HTML canvas. The player’s silhouette runs automatically; clicking/tapping makes it jump, a second tap makes it slide. Random obstacles (spikes, walls, gaps) appear.  
**Lose condition:** colliding with any obstacle or falling into a gap ends the game. The goal is to survive as long as possible while collecting glowing orbs for points.
