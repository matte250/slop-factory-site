# Game Idea: Shadow Dash

A side‑scrolling endless runner rendered on an HTML canvas. The player’s silhouette runs automatically; clicking or tapping makes it jump, and a second tap makes it slide. Obstacles (spikes, walls, gaps) appear randomly. **Lose condition:** colliding with any obstacle or falling into a gap ends the game. The goal is to survive as long as possible while collecting glowing orbs for points.
