Added simple sound effects using the Web Audio API:

- **Audio context & helper** – `audioCtx` with `playTone` to generate tones.
- **Sound wrappers** – `shootSound`, `explosionSound`, `gameOverSound`.
- **Triggers** – firing a projectile, destroying a meteor, and game‑over now play the respective sounds, with the audio context resumed on user interaction.

All changes are within `game.js` and preserve existing gameplay.
