Implemented sound effects:

- Created an `AudioContext` with a helper `playTone(freq, dur)` to generate simple tones.
- Resumes the audio context on the first key press to satisfy browser autoplay policies.
- Plays a high‑pitched tone when the ship collects an orb.
- Plays a low‑pitched tone when the ship collides with a meteor.

These additions keep the gameplay unchanged while adding auditory feedback.
