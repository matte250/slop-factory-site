Added sound effects to the game:

- **Audio context** created and resumed on any user interaction.  
- **playSound** helper generates simple sine‑wave tones.  
- Jump now triggers a short high‑pitch sound.  
- Each time the score increments, a subtle “ding” is played.  
- Game‑over plays a low‑pitch tone.  

All changes are in `game.js` and work with the existing `<canvas id="game">`.
