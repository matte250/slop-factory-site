Implemented **Neon Runner** game in `game.js`:

- Targets the canvas element with `id="game"`.
- Handles canvas resizing, player physics (gravity, jump), and input (spacebar or click/tap).
- Generates scrolling world segments with random gaps and spikes.
- Detects collisions with spikes or gaps to trigger game over.
- Updates score and high score, persisting the high score in `localStorage`.
- Renders background, ground, spikes, player, and UI (score/high score, game‑over overlay).
