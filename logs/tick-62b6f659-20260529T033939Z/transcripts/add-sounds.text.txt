Implemented audio feedback:

* Created an `AudioContext` and a helper `playBeep(freq)` that plays a short sine‑wave beep.
* On each arrow‑key press, the corresponding note (different frequencies per direction) is triggered.
* When the player circle collides with the canvas edge, a higher‑pitched beep (600 Hz) plays.
* Added logic to resume the audio context if it’s suspended.

Graphics were also refined (starfield background, gradient player), keeping the game visually engaging while now providing sound cues for movement and collisions.
