## Concept
The player watches a dot continuously racing forward through an endless scrolling tunnel. By tapping to slide a vertical curtain left or right, they create a safe gap for the dot to slip through as obstacles sweep by, delivering a satisfying rhythm of timing and reflex.

## Player
The player controls a vertical curtain that spans the tunnel height and can be slid horizontally. Visually it appears as a moving panel that reveals a narrow opening. Verbs: slide left, slide right, tap.

## Core loop
Every 1–3 seconds a new barrier approaches the dot. The player instantly reads the upcoming gap’s position, slides the curtain to align the opening, and watches the dot auto‑advance through it. Successful passes reward points; mis‑alignment triggers a collision and ends the run. The loop repeats, increasing speed and narrowing gaps.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Static barriers**: solid walls with a single rectangular gap that moves toward the player.
- **Moving gaps**: barriers whose gap slides left or right while the wall approaches, forcing the curtain to track.
- **Multi‑gap sections**: occasional walls with two gaps, rewarding a precise choice of curtain side.
All hazards travel horizontally across the tunnel, disappearing after passing the dot.

## Score
The score increments each time the dot safely passes a barrier. Bonus points are awarded for passing when the gap is particularly narrow or for consecutive flawless passes.

## Win and lose
The player loses when the dot collides with any solid part of a barrier, triggering a “Game Over” overlay showing the final score and restart instructions. The player wins after successfully navigating a predefined number of barriers (e.g., twenty) or surviving for a set duration, prompting a celebratory “You Win!” screen.

## Feel
Frantic, high‑tempo arcade action set in a neon‑lit, dark tunnel. Visuals use glowing cyan and magenta accents against a deep black backdrop, with pulsing synth‑style chiptune audio that accentuates each slide and collision.

## Why it's fun
Because a single tap can instantly move the curtain, and the thrill of watching the dot narrowly escape a solid wall creates that perfect “just‑in‑time” rush.
