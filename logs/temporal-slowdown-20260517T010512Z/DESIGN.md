## Concept
The player guides a constantly moving dot through an endless tunnel, tapping to briefly slow time and thread tighter gaps.

## Player
The player controls a luminous dot that automatically advances forward through the tunnel. The only verb is "tap" (or click), which triggers a temporal slowdown.

## Core loop
The dot slides forward while obstacles scroll toward the player. The player watches the upcoming tunnel, decides when a gap is too narrow, and taps to slow the world, allowing the dot to slip through. After the slowdown ends, normal speed resumes and new obstacles appear, prompting the next decision.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- Moving walls that swing inward, creating narrow passages.
- Rotating shafts that spin faster than the dot’s speed, requiring precise timing.
- Pulsating barriers that expand and contract, opening only briefly.
- Static spikes that protrude from the tunnel floor or ceiling; they are harmless during slowdown but deadly at normal speed.

## Score
- Each obstacle successfully passed during a slowdown.
- Surviving a wave of obstacles without a slowdown.
- Collecting occasional glowing orbs that appear only during the slowdown.

## Win and lose
The player loses when the dot collides with any hazard while time is normal. Upon death, a “Game Over” overlay displays the final score and prompts a restart. The player wins by achieving a high score threshold or surviving a predefined number of waves, after which a celebratory “You Win!” overlay appears.

## Feel
A tense, rhythmic experience with a steady forward thrust interrupted by sharp, gratifying bursts of slowed time. A neon‑lit, futuristic tunnel aesthetic with a synth‑wave soundtrack enhances the sense of urgency and control.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of snapping time to the exact moment a narrow gap opens, letting the dot glide through just as the world rushes back to speed.
