Added sound effects:

- Created an `AudioContext` with a reusable `playTone` helper.
- Added collision and win frequencies (`COLLISION_FREQ`, `WIN_FREQ`).
- Resumes the audio context on the first key press (required for browsers).
- Plays a short sine tone on win (higher pitch) and on collision (lower pitch).

All changes are in `game.js`; the game now provides audible feedback for success and failure while keeping the existing gameplay and graphics.
