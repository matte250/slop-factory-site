# Game Idea: Canvas Runner
- A side‑scrolling endless runner where the player controls a square that automatically runs to the right.  
- The player can tap/press space to jump over gaps or obstacles that appear randomly.  
- **Lose condition:** Collide with an obstacle or fall into a gap, ending the run.  
- Simple controls and graphics make it straightforward to implement with the HTML `<canvas>` API.
