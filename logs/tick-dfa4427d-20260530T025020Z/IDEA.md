# Game Idea: Asteroid Escape

- **Genre:** Top‑down arcade
- **Core Loop:** Use arrow keys to steer a ship through a constantly scrolling field of asteroids.
- **Goal:** Collect fuel cells to stay powered.
- **Lose Condition:** Collision with an asteroid **or** running out of fuel (timer reaches zero).
- **Canvas Features:** Simple sprite drawing, per‑frame movement, collision detection, and a timer UI.
