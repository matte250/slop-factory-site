## Concept
The player guides a glowing dot down an endless tunnel whose floor tilts left or right. Each tap flips the tilt, sending the dot sliding the opposite way to thread through side gaps, creating a rhythm of anticipation and quick reaction.

## Player
A small glowing dot that continuously slides down the tunnel. The player’s only direct action is to tap (or click) to flip the tunnel’s tilt, indirectly steering the dot left or right.

## Core loop
The dot slides down the sloping tunnel while gaps appear alternately on the left and right walls. The player watches the approaching gap, decides the optimal moment, and taps to flip the tilt, sending the dot toward the open space. Successful navigation through each gap rewards the player and immediately presents the next challenge.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- Solid tunnel walls that the dot collides with if it misses a gap.
- Spike protrusions that extend from the floor or ceiling, appearing in the dot’s path.
- Sweeping horizontal barriers that move across the tunnel, requiring timing to avoid.

## Score
The score increments each time the dot successfully passes through a side gap. Additional points are earned for collecting floating orbs that drift through the tunnel.

## Win and lose
The player loses when the dot collides with a wall, spike, or barrier, triggering a game‑over screen. The player wins by reaching the tunnel’s exit after successfully navigating a continuous series of gaps.

## Feel
A fast‑paced, reflex‑driven experience with neon‑bright visuals against a dark backdrop, accented by crisp chiptune beeps that echo each tilt and collision. The gameplay feels rhythmic yet urgent, urging the player to stay on the edge of timing.

## Why it's fun
Flipping the slope at the perfect instant to thread the dot through a narrowing gap delivers a sharp, satisfying payoff that feels both tense and rewarding.
