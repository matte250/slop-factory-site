Added basic sound effects using the Web Audio API:

* **Audio context** created once and resumed on the first key press.  
* Helper `playTone` generates short tones with configurable frequency, duration, and waveform.  
* Specific sound functions:
  * `playCollect()` – high‑pitched beep when a beacon is collected.  
  * `playExplosion()` – lower triangle wave for asteroid collisions (game over).  
  * `playThrust()` – quick burst while the ship moves upward.  
* Integrated calls:
  * Play thrust sound each time the **up** arrow moves the ship.  
  * Play collect sound on beacon pickup.  
  * Play explosion sound on asteroid collision.

All changes are inside `game.js`; the game now includes auditory feedback while preserving existing mechanics and graphics.
