## Game Idea: Space Debris Dodge

**Concept**: The player pilots a small spaceship on an HTML canvas, moving left/right and up/down to avoid falling pieces of space debris that appear from the top. The debris speed and density increase over time.

**Controls**: Arrow keys or WASD to move the ship.

**Goal**: Survive as long as possible, accumulating a score based on time survived.

**Lose Condition**: The game ends immediately when the ship collides with any debris.
