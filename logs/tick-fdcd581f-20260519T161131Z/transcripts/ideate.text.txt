**IDEA.md** created with a concise HTML‑canvas game concept:

# Asteroid Dodge

Control a spacecraft at the bottom of the canvas using arrow keys or mouse.  
Asteroids fall from the top, speeding up over time.  
Collect occasional power‑ups to boost speed or clear a row.  

**Lose condition:** The game ends when an asteroid collides with the ship.  

Goal: survive as long as possible and achieve a high score.
