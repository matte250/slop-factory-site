## Concept
One short sentence: The player navigates a vessel and taps to unleash a brief expanding shockwave that clears nearby obstacles, carving a safe corridor that must be used sparingly because it recharges.

## Player
The player controls a small ship/marker that glides around the play area. It is visually represented as a glowing point or minimalist icon. Verbs: move (freely in any direction), burst (tap or click to emit a shockwave), idle (choose when not to burst).

## Core loop
Every few seconds, obstacles stream toward the player while the player constantly steers to avoid them. The player watches the approaching threats, judging the optimal moment to tap and generate a shockwave that instantly wipes out anything within its radius, then endures the cooldown before the next burst. The cycle of navigation, threat assessment, and timed bursts repeats, driving a rhythm of tension and release.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Linear projectiles**: fast‑moving objects that travel straight across the screen; they can be destroyed by the shockwave.
- **Homing drones**: entities that adjust their trajectory toward the player; they are vulnerable to the burst but otherwise chase relentlessly.
- **Static barriers**: stationary blocks that block movement; they shatter only when hit by a shockwave.
- **Swarm clusters**: groups of small debris that drift together, creating dense fields the player must carve through with a burst.
Each hazard presents a distinct visual cue and movement pattern, encouraging the player to time bursts strategically.

## Score
Score increases when the player:
- Destroys obstacles with a shockwave (more points for larger groups).
- Survives a wave of incoming threats without taking damage.
- Completes a burst without wasting it on empty space (precision bonus).
The score is displayed constantly, giving immediate feedback for each successful clearance.

## Win and lose
The player loses instantly on collision with any obstacle. On death, a Game Over overlay shows the final score and prompts a restart.
The player wins by surviving a predefined number of threat waves (e.g., ten) or reaching a target score, after which a Victory screen celebrates the accomplishment and offers to play again.

## Feel
Frantic yet precise: fast‑moving threats keep the pace urgent, while the brief, luminous shockwave provides a satisfying moment of visual release. A neon‑lit, cyber‑punk aesthetic with glowing outlines and pulse‑synchronized sound effects reinforces the high‑energy arcade vibe.

## Why it's fun
The hook is the exhilarating contrast of tight, life‑threatening navigation and the powerful instant blast that clears a path, making players grin when a perfectly timed tap turns a near‑certain death into a dramatic escape.
