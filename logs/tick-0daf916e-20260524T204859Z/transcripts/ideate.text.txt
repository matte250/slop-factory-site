# Random Game Idea: Color Switch Escape

A minimalist HTML canvas game where the player controls a small circle that constantly moves forward. The canvas is filled with rotating arcs and barriers in different colors. The player's circle can change its color by tapping the screen (or pressing a key), cycling through the available colors. The circle must pass through gaps that match its current color; colliding with a barrier of a different color causes an instant loss. The level speed gradually increases, and the goal is to survive as long as possible while aiming for a high score.

**Lose condition:** Collision with a barrier of a mismatched color or moving off the canvas bounds.
