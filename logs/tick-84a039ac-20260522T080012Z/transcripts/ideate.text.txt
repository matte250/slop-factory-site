# Random Canvas Game Idea

**Title:** Asteroid Escape  

**Concept:**  
- Player pilots a ship on an HTML canvas, moving with arrow keys (or WASD).  
- Randomly generated asteroids drift across the screen, varying in size and speed.  
- New asteroids continuously spawn.  
- The ship has a limited fuel meter that depletes over time; occasional fuel canisters can be collected to refill it.  

**Lose Conditions:**  
- Colliding with an asteroid.  
- Fuel meter reaching zero.  

**Goal:** Survive as long as possible, earning a high score based on time survived and fuel canisters collected.
