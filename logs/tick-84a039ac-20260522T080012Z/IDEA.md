# Random Canvas Game Idea

**Title:** Asteroid Escape

**Concept:**
- Player pilots a small ship on an HTML canvas.
- The ship moves with arrow keys (or WASD) to dodge randomly generated asteroids that drift across the screen.
- Asteroids vary in size and speed, and new ones spawn continuously.
- The ship has a limited fuel meter that depletes over time; fuel can be refilled by collecting occasional fuel canisters.

**Lose Conditions:**
- Collision with any asteroid.
- Fuel meter reaches zero.

**Goal:** Survive as long as possible and achieve a high score based on time survived and fuel canisters collected.
