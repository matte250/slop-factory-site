Added sound effects to **game.js**:

* Defined three tiny base64‑encoded WAVs for **star**, **lose**, and **win** events.  
* Played `sounds.star` on star collection, `sounds.lose` on collision, and `sounds.win` when the shrinking timer expires.  

All audio is loaded via `new Audio(...)` and triggered at the appropriate points, preserving existing gameplay and graphics.
