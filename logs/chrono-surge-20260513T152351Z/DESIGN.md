## Concept
The player guides a glowing dot hurtling through an endless tunnel. By tapping, they trigger a brief time‑dilation burst that propels the dot forward while the tunnel temporarily slows, creating a short window to slip through tight clusters of obstacles. After each burst a short cooldown prevents immediate reuse.

## Player
A small, bright dot represents the player’s avatar. It constantly advances forward through the scrolling tunnel. Primary verb: **tap** (or click) to activate a time‑dilation burst. The dot may also be steered laterally to aim for gaps, but the core mechanic revolves around the burst activation.

## Core loop
Every 1–3 seconds the tunnel scrolls obstacles toward the player. The player watches the approaching pattern, decides whether to tap, and if they do, the burst sends the dot ahead while the tunnel decelerates, giving a momentary slow‑motion gap to navigate. The burst ends, a cooldown begins, and new obstacles appear, repeating the cycle with increasing density.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Static spikes**: Fixed protrusions that block the tunnel’s path; colliding ends the run.
- **Sweeping barriers**: Horizontal blocks that move across the tunnel, creating a moving gap that must be timed.
- **Rotating rings**: Rings that spin, opening a rotating passage that aligns only at certain angles.
- **Dense clusters**: Groups of small obstacles packed tightly, demanding precise burst timing to thread through.
Each hazard threatens collision, forcing the player to choose the right moment for a burst.

## Score
Score increments when the player:
- successfully completes a burst through a cluster,
- passes an obstacle without collision,
- activates a burst that results in a safe passage.
Collectible “time crystals” appear during bursts; gathering one adds a bonus point.

## Win and lose
The player loses instantly on any collision with an obstacle. The game ends with a “Game Over” overlay showing the final score and restart prompt. The player wins by surviving a set duration (e.g., one minute) or reaching a high‑score milestone, triggering a celebratory “You Survived!” screen.

## Feel
Frantic bursts contrast with a steady, rhythmic tunnel flow, creating a push‑pull rhythm. Neon‑glow visuals against a dark background give a cyber‑punk aesthetic, while synth‑wave pulses and sharp burst sounds reinforce the temporal distortion theme.

## Why it's fun
The single‑tap timing of a time‑dilation burst offers a satisfying rhythm: perfectly timing a burst to thread through an impossible‑looking cluster delivers a quick, exhilarating moment of triumph.
