Implemented audio:

- Created an `AudioContext` and a simple `playSound(freq, duration)` helper.
- Resumes the audio context on the first key press (required for browsers).
- Plays a short “debris spawn” tone (300 Hz) each time a new debris object appears.
- Plays a deeper “collision” tone (100 Hz) when the satellite collides with debris.

All changes are in **game.js**, enhancing the experience with sound effects.
