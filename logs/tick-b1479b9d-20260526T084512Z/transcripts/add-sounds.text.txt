Added sound effects using the Web Audio API:

* **Collision** – low‑tone beep when the drone hits an asteroid.  
* **Collect** – higher‑pitch beep when a gem is collected.  
* **Game Over** – longer low‑tone when hull reaches zero.  

Audio context is resumed on the first key press to satisfy browser autoplay policies. The rest of the game logic and graphics remain unchanged. Include the script as before (`<script src="game.js"></script>`) and the sounds will play during gameplay.
