# Game Idea: Neon Runner

A fast‑paced top‑down endless runner on an HTML canvas. The player controls a neon square that moves forward automatically and can jump over or slide under incoming obstacle blocks. Obstacles appear at random positions and speeds. The canvas scrolls continuously, increasing difficulty over time.

**Lose condition:** The game ends when the player collides with an obstacle or falls off the bottom of the screen.
