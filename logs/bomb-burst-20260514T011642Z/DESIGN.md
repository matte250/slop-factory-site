## Concept
The player taps (or clicks) to drop a timed bomb that clears any obstacles inside its blast radius, carving a safe path through dense hazard fields. Timing each explosion to create just‑right openings creates a visceral, satisfying loop.

## Player
The player controls a small avatar—represented as a cursor/ship—able to move freely across the play area and drop bombs at any location. Verbs: move, drop bomb.

## Core loop
Obstacles continuously stream across the screen, forming tight corridors. The player watches their approach, moves to a safe spot, and chooses when to drop a bomb. After a short delay the bomb detonates, erasing any obstacles within its radius, instantly opening a gap. The player then rushes through the newly cleared passage before the next wave arrives. Each decision to drop a bomb feels urgent, and the moment of the explosion provides immediate visual reward.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Dense moving blocks** that travel in predictable patterns, filling the screen and forcing the player to find a gap.
- **Rotating sweepers** that spin around a pivot, carving temporary safe arcs but sweeping through the player’s position if mistimed.
- **Static spikes** that appear in clusters and cannot be moved through; they can only be removed by a bomb’s blast.
- **Rapid projectiles** that zip across the field, providing a timing challenge for bomb placement.

All hazards threaten the player on contact and must be cleared or avoided.

## Score
- Clearing any obstacle with a bomb adds points.
- Surviving a wave without taking damage grants a bonus.
- Performing a chain of bomb detonations that clear multiple obstacles in quick succession yields a combo multiplier.

## Win and lose
The player loses when an obstacle contacts the avatar, triggering a game‑over overlay. The player wins by reaching the end of a predefined stage or by surviving a set duration while clearing all hazards, at which point a celebratory “You Win!” screen appears.

## Feel
Fast‑paced, tension‑filled gameplay with bright neon bursts against a dark backdrop. A synth‑driven chiptune soundtrack underscores each explosion, while visual feedback—flashing colors and particle bursts—makes every cleared path feel rewarding.

## Why it's fun
The hook is the instant, dramatic payoff when a perfectly timed bomb clears a wall of danger, giving the player a triumphant burst of space to sprint through.
