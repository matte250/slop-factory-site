### Neon Runner (HTML Canvas)

- **Genre:** Infinite runner on a scrolling neon cityscape.
- **Controls:** Arrow keys or tap to jump/slide.
- **Goal:** Survive as long as possible, earning points based on distance traveled.
- **Lose condition:** Colliding with any obstacle (building, gap, or neon sign) ends the game.
- **Features:** Simple Canvas graphics, procedural obstacle generation, high‑score tracking.
