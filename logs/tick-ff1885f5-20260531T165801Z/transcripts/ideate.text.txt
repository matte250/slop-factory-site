**IDEA.md** has been created with a concise game concept:

- **Title:** Asteroid Escape  
- **Genre:** Endless side‑scroll runner.  
- **Gameplay:** Ship moves up/down (arrow keys or mouse) while the canvas scrolls leftward. Random asteroids appear from the right.  
- **Goal:** Survive as long as possible; score is distance traveled.  
- **Lose Condition:** Collision with an asteroid or moving outside the canvas’s vertical bounds.
