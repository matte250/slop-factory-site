Implemented enhanced graphics and added sound effects:

* **Starfield background** with twinkling stars.
* **Rotating, gradient‑filled asteroids** for a richer look.
* **Glowing particle explosion** on crash.
* **Gradient‑filled fuel orbs**.
* **Audio context** with reusable `playSound`, `playThrust`, `playExplosion`, and `playCollect` functions.
* Thrust, collision, and orb‑collect events now trigger appropriate sounds.
* Game loop pauses after game‑over until particles finish, then allows restart.
