# Game Idea: **Asteroid Escape**

- **Genre:** Endless runner on an HTML canvas.
- **Core mechanic:** The player pilots a small ship that automatically scrolls forward through a field of randomly generated asteroids. Tapping/clicking applies thrust to dodge.
- **Goal:** Survive as long as possible while collecting glowing fuel orbs to increase score.
- **Lose condition:** The game ends immediately when the ship collides with any asteroid.
- **Features:** Simple graphics, particle explosion on crash, progressive speed increase, and a high‑score table.
