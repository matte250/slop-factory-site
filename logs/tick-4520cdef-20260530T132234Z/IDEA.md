# Game Idea

**Title:** Asteroid Dodge

**Concept:** The player pilots a small ship on an HTML canvas and must dodge an endless stream of randomly generated asteroids. The ship can rotate and thrust forward. Asteroids vary in size and speed, and they spawn from the edges of the canvas and move across.

**Lose Condition:** The game ends when the ship collides with any asteroid.

**Goal:** Survive as long as possible and achieve the highest score based on time survived and asteroids dodged.
