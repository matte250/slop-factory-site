Added audio feedback:

- **Engine thrust** sound on upward thrust.
- **Laser** sound when firing.
- **Mining hit** sound on asteroid mining.
- **Collision** sound when the ship hits an asteroid.
- **Game‑over** sound plays once when fuel or hull depletes.

Implemented via the Web Audio API, with a simple `playTone` helper and wrappers for each event. Updated input handling to resume the audio context on first interaction and added a flag to ensure the game‑over sound triggers only once. Graphics remain unchanged.
