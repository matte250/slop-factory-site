## Concept
The player controls a dot tethered to a fixed point ahead of a scrolling tunnel. The dot swings like a pendulum under gravity, and each tap gives an impulse that changes the swing direction, letting the player rhythmically time arcs to slip through gaps and dodge obstacles. The core pleasure comes from mastering the swing’s momentum and timing.

## Player
A glowing dot attached by a visible tether to a static anchor point in the tunnel.
Verbs: swing (by physics), impulse (tap to add thrust), dodge (avoid obstacles), survive (stay alive).

## Core loop
The tunnel continuously scrolls, presenting a stream of gaps and obstacles. While the dot naturally swings, the player can tap to add an impulse that alters the arc. The player watches the upcoming layout, decides the precise moment to tap, and hopes the new trajectory carries the dot through the next hazard. This rhythm repeats every few seconds, with the difficulty gradually increasing.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Gaps** in the floor or ceiling that require the dot to be at the right height to pass.
- **Horizontal sweeps**: spikes that slide across the tunnel, telegraphing their path before crossing the swing line.
- **Rotating blades** fixed to the walls, spinning at a constant rate and intersecting the pendulum’s path.
- **Magnetic pulls**: zones that briefly attract the dot toward a wall, disrupting its swing.

## Score
Score increases each time the player clears an obstacle or gap without collision. Bonus points are awarded for consecutive clear passes, rewarding precise timing.

## Win and lose
The player loses when the dot collides with any obstacle or is pulled out of the tunnel. On death, a “Game Over” overlay appears with the final score and a prompt to restart. The player wins by surviving a set distance or a fixed number of obstacle waves, triggering a celebratory “You Win!” screen.

## Feel
A steady, pulse‑driven rhythm with a neon‑glow aesthetic; the background hum of synth tones reacts to each tap, while subtle impact sounds mark successful passes and ominous cues warn of incoming hazards. The overall pace feels tense yet controllable, encouraging focused, rhythmic play.

## Why it's fun
Timing each tap to catch the perfect swing arc and thread through tight gaps delivers a instant “aha!” moment that makes players grin, curse, and keep trying for a higher score.
