```
## Concept
The player taps to unleash an expanding shockwave that pushes nearby obstacles outward, briefly clearing a corridor for the dot to advance. The satisfaction comes from instantly reshaping the field and timing bursts to forge a path.

## Player
The player controls a small glowing dot that drifts forward. Visually it is a tiny neon sphere. Its verbs are:
- **tap** – emit a shockwave centered on the dot.
- **move** – the dot drifts forward automatically (or can be nudged).

## Core loop
Every 1–3 seconds the dot approaches a cluster of obstacles. The player watches the incoming hazards, decides when to tap, and watches the shockwave push obstacles aside, creating a fleeting gap. The dot then moves through the gap, the wave dissipates, and the cycle repeats, rewarding timing and anticipation.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Sliding blocks** that glide horizontally or vertically, colliding with the dot if not displaced.
- **Rotating spinners** that spin in place and extend outward, threatening the dot’s path.
- **Static walls** that sit in the arena; when struck by a shockwave they are pushed temporarily before resettling.
- **Bursting mines** that remain dormant until the shockwave reaches them, then explode outward, forcing the player to time bursts carefully.

All obstacles are pushed outward by the shockwave for a brief moment, then resume their original motion.

## Score
- Each time the dot successfully passes through a cleared corridor.
- Each obstacle displaced by a shockwave without colliding with the dot.
- Bonus points for chaining multiple taps without being hit.

## Win and lose
- **Lose**: An obstacle contacts the dot; the game shows “Game Over” and the final score.
- **Win**: The dot reaches a predefined endpoint (e.g., the far side of the arena) or survives a set number of obstacle waves. The win screen celebrates with a burst of neon and the final score.

## Feel
Frantic, neon‑lit arcade action with a pulse‑driven rhythm. The visual style is high‑contrast neon on a dark background, complemented by crisp arcade‑style blips and a low‑frequency hum. The experience feels like a rapid, reactive dance of light and motion.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of timing a single tap to blast obstacles aside, watching the shockwave ripple and the dot surge forward in the newly cleared path.
```
