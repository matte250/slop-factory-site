Implemented a concise **Cosmic Collector** game in `game.js`. The script:

- Targets the canvas with ID `game`.
- Handles player movement (arrow keys/WASD), fuel depletion, and boundary constraints.
- Spawns random debris (collect for points) and asteroids (collision ends game).
- Updates game state, checks collisions, and renders ship, debris, asteroids, and HUD.
- Ends when fuel runs out or an asteroid hits the ship.
