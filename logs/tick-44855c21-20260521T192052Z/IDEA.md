# Random Game Idea: Orbit Dodger

**Concept**: Player pilots a small ship that orbits a planet in an HTML canvas. Asteroids and debris drift inwards on curved trajectories. The ship can thrust forward or reverse to adjust its orbit and collect occasional power‑ups.

**Lose Condition**: The game ends when the ship collides with an asteroid/debris or runs out of fuel.

**Core Loop**: Continuous orbiting, dodge incoming obstacles, manage fuel, and aim for a high survival time score.
