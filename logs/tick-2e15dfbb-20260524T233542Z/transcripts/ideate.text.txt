```
# Game Idea

- **Title:** Neon Runner
- **Genre:** Endless runner / avoidance
- **Core:** Player controls a small circle moving horizontally across a scrolling HTML canvas, dodging incoming rectangular obstacles that appear from the right. Speed gradually increases.
- **Lose condition:** Collision with any obstacle ends the game.
```
