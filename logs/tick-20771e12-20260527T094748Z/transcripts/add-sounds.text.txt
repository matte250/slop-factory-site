Added richer graphics and sound:

* **Audio** – Web Audio API with `playThrust` (short burst) and `playExplosion` (longer boom). Thrust sound triggers on click/tap; explosion sound plays on game‑over.
* **Background** – vertical space gradient and a 100‑star field that scrolls for parallax.
* **Ship** – drawn as a green triangle.
* **Asteroids** – now have random rotation and angular velocity, rendered with radial gradients and rotation for a 3‑D effect.
* **Stars** move slowly leftward to simulate depth.

All changes are in `game.js` and integrate with the existing canvas (`id="game"`). The game now has dynamic visuals and audio feedback while preserving original mechanics.
