Implemented sound effects:

* Created an `AudioContext` and helper `playTone` for simple synthesized tones.
* Added specific sound functions:
  * `soundCollision` – asteroid hit without shield.
  * `soundShield` – shield activation or shield‑break.
  * `soundPowerUp` – when a power‑up is collected.
  * `soundGameOver` – on game over.
* Resumed the audio context on the first key press, ensuring playback works after user interaction.
* Integrated sounds into gameplay:
  * Play collision and game‑over sounds when the ship hits an asteroid without a shield.
  * Play shield sound when a shield‑protected hit destroys an asteroid.
  * Play power‑up and shield activation sounds when a shield power‑up is collected.

All changes are in **game.js**; the game now has auditory feedback for key events.
