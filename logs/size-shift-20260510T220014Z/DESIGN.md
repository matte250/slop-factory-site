## Concept
The player taps to toggle a dot between a tiny and a large radius, threading through narrow gaps when small and smashing weak barriers or reaching distant platforms when large. Rapid, rhythmic size changes create a satisfying tension‑and‑release loop.

## Player
A glowing dot that can be tiny or large. Verbs: **move** (directional input), **toggle size** (tap or key press). The avatar is a simple luminous sphere, visually minimal.

## Core loop
Every few seconds the player encounters a new segment of obstacles. They read the shape of the upcoming gap or barrier, decide whether to stay small to slip through or grow large to break through, then toggle size at the right moment while moving forward. Success rewards a visual burst and a point; failure ends the run. The loop repeats, increasing the frequency and variety of size‑decision moments.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Narrow gaps**: static walls with tight openings that only a tiny dot can pass.
- **Fragile barriers**: blocks that shatter when the player is large, disappearing after a smash.
- **Moving spikes**: horizontal or vertical sweepers that punish mistimed size changes.
- **Falling shards**: projectiles that rain down; they can be avoided by staying small or destroyed by being large.
- **Timed platforms**: floating platforms that appear only when the player is large, allowing jumps to higher sections.

## Score
- **Gap slip**: each successful pass through a narrow gap adds a point.
- **Barrier smash**: each shattered fragile barrier adds a point.
- **Distance**: traveling a set segment distance awards a bonus.
- **Combo**: consecutive correct size toggles without a miss grant extra points.

## Win and lose
- **Win**: Reach the end of the level (the final platform) and trigger a "You Win" burst.
- **Lose**: Collide with a barrier while the dot is the wrong size, or be hit by a moving spike. The screen flashes, displays "Game Over" with the final score, and shows restart instructions.

## Feel
Frantic, pulse‑driven gameplay with a neon‑lit, cyber‑punk aesthetic. Dark background contrasted by bright cyan and magenta glows. Tight, syncopated chiptune beats accent each toggle, with sharp sound effects for smashing and slipping.

## Why it's fun
The instant joy of perfectly timing a size change to zip through an impossible gap or crush an obstacle makes the player smile, curse, and shout for one more try.
