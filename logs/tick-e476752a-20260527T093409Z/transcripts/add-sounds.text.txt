Sounds have been added:

* **AudioContext** is created and resumed on the first key press.  
* **Turn actions** (←/→) emit a short 400 Hz beep.  
* **Collision** plays a low‑pitched 150 Hz beep before the game‑over overlay.  

All audio handling is self‑contained in `game.js` and works with the existing `<canvas id="game">`.
