# Game Idea: "Pixel Runner"

- Side‑scrolling endless runner rendered on an HTML canvas.  
- Player controls a pixel character that can jump (space) and slide (down) to avoid obstacles and gaps.  
- Obstacles randomly spawn; speed gradually increases.  
- **Lose condition:** collision with an obstacle or falling into a gap.  
- Simple graphics, score based on distance, high‑score saved locally.
