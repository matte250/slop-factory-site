**Game Idea: Cosmic Runner**  
- Endless side‑scrolling runner on an HTML canvas.  
- Player controls a ship that thrusts upward when clicked/tapped, fighting gravity.  
- Random asteroids and laser beams appear from the right.  
- **Lose condition:** collision with any obstacle or touching the bottom edge of the canvas.  
- Goal: survive as long as possible, collecting stars for a high score.
