# Canvas Escape

A minimalist endless‑runner where the player controls a small circle that drifts through a side‑scrolling field of rotating rectangular obstacles. Each obstacle has a gap; the player must steer through the gaps using arrow keys. The game ends (lose condition) when the circle collides with an obstacle or leaves the canvas bounds. Score increases with distance traveled. Simple shapes and animations make it perfect for HTML canvas.
