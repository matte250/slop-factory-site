# Game Idea: Space Debris Dodge

- **Genre**: Arcade / Endless runner
- **Controls**: Arrow keys (left/right) to move a small spaceship horizontally across the bottom of the canvas.
- **Gameplay**: Randomly generated asteroids and debris fall from the top at increasing speed. The player must dodge them; the ship can also fire a single laser to destroy a nearby piece (cooldown 2 seconds).
- **Lose Condition**: The game ends when any debris collides with the ship.
- **Goal**: Survive as long as possible, with the score equal to the time survived in seconds.
- **Canvas Features**: Use simple shapes (triangles for ship, circles for debris) and `requestAnimationFrame` for smooth animation.

*Simple, self‑contained, and perfect for a single‑page HTML Canvas project.*
