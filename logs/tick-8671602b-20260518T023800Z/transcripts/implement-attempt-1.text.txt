Implemented **game.js** with a full Space Debris Dodge arcade game:

- Targets canvas with `id="game"`.
- Handles resizing, keyboard controls (←/→ to move, Space to fire laser with 2 s cooldown).
- Spawns increasing debris, updates positions, and checks collisions.
- Provides score (time survived) and game‑over overlay.
- Uses simple shapes (triangle ship, circular debris) and `requestAnimationFrame` for smooth animation.
