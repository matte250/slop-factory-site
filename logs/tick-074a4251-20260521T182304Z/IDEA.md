# Game Idea: Canvas Escape

**Concept**: The player controls a small dot that must navigate through an ever‑shrinking maze of moving walls on an HTML canvas. The walls slide horizontally and vertically, leaving narrow gaps that the dot can pass through.

**Objective**: Survive as long as possible by continuously moving the dot through the gaps.

**Lose Condition**: If the dot collides with any wall or is trapped when the maze contracts completely, the game ends.

**Controls**: Arrow keys or WASD to move the dot in four directions.

**Features**: Simple graphics, increasing difficulty over time, optional high‑score tracking.
