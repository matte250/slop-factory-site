Implemented **`game.js`**:

- Targets the canvas with `id="game"` and sets up rendering context.
- Defines player physics (gravity, jump) and endless‑runner mechanics.
- Generates random obstacles (spikes or low bars) that scroll left.
- Handles collision detection, game‑over overlay, and input (click/touch to jump).
- Starts the main game loop with `requestAnimationFrame`.
