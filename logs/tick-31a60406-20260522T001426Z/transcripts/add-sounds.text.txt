Implemented richer graphics and added sound effects:

- **Background:** vertical gradient from sky blue to light cyan.
- **Audio:** created `AudioContext`, a `playTone` helper, and integrated jump and collision sounds.
- **Jump:** plays a short tone when the player jumps.
- **Collision:** plays a lower‑pitch tone on game‑over.

All changes are in `game.js`, targeting the canvas with `id="game"`.
