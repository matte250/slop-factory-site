# Random Canvas Game Idea

**Title:** Meteor Escape

**Concept:** The player controls a spaceship constantly moving forward in a starfield. Meteors spawn from the top and travel toward the ship. The player can move left/right and boost up/down to dodge. Collect power‑ups for temporary shields or speed.

**Lose Condition:** The ship is destroyed on collision with a meteor while no shield is active, triggering a Game Over screen.

**Goal:** Survive as long as possible and earn a high score based on time and collected power‑ups.
