# Game Idea: Canvas Escape

- **Genre:** Arcade puzzle platformer on an HTML5 canvas.
- **Core mechanic:** A small shape continuously falls through a vertical scrolling tunnel filled with moving obstacles (spikes, rotating bars, gaps).
- **Player control:** Move left/right and briefly boost upward to dodge.
- **Lose condition:** Colliding with any obstacle or reaching the bottom of the screen (i.e., failing to stay above a falling line).
- **Goal:** Survive as long as possible, accumulating points for distance traveled.
