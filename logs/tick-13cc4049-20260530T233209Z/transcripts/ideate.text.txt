**IDEA.md** has been created with a concise HTML canvas game concept:

- **Game:** *Meteor Dodge* – a top‑down endless game where the player pilots a spaceship, dodging randomly spawning meteors.  
- **Controls:** Rotate with left/right arrows, thrust with up arrow.  
- **Mechanics:** Meteors increase in speed; fuel depletes over time but can be replenished via floating canisters.  
- **Lose condition:** Collision with a meteor **or** fuel reaching zero.  
- **Score:** Time survived.  

The idea is fully described in `IDEA.md` at the working directory root.
