Implemented **Cosmic Cleaner** game in `game.js`:

- Targets canvas with id `game`.
- Handles ship movement (arrow keys) and vacuum toggle (space bar).
- Spawns asteroids and collectible debris.
- Implements collision, cargo collection, overload death, and a simple cargo‑dump shortcut (`d`).
- Renders ship, beam, objects, and HUD with score and game‑over message.
- Runs an animation loop using `requestAnimationFrame`.
