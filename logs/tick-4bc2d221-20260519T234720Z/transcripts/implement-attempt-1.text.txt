Implemented a minimal endless‑runner game in **`game.js`** that targets the canvas with `id="game"`.

- Sets up canvas size, player object, and simple physics (jump & slide).  
- Generates random spikes and gaps as obstacles.  
- Handles input: space/arrow‑up to jump, arrow‑down to slide, click to jump.  
- Updates movement, collision detection, scoring, and drawing each frame.  
- Shows “Game Over” overlay when the player collides or falls into a gap.  

The script runs automatically when loaded and provides a functional, concise game based on the IDEA.md description.
