```
# Game Idea: Asteroid Dodge

- HTML‑canvas top‑down game where the player pilots a ship that can move freely.
- Asteroids spawn from the canvas edges and travel inward, accelerating over time.
- The ship can collect occasional shields for brief invulnerability.
- **Lose condition:** Collision with any asteroid or being pushed outside the canvas bounds.
```
