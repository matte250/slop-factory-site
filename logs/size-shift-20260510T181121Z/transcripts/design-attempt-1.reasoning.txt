## Concept
The player guides a dot that constantly moves forward through a scrolling tunnel; each tap switches the dot between a small and a large radius, letting it slip through tight gaps or block wide obstacles that match its current size. The satisfying rhythm comes from quickly reading the tunnel and timing size changes.

## Player
The avatar is a single, glowing dot that travels forward automatically. The player can tap/click (or press a key) to toggle the dot’s radius between two fixed sizes. This size toggle is the only direct control.

## Core loop
Every few seconds the tunnel scrolls forward, revealing a new segment with a pattern of narrow and wide passages. The player watches the upcoming shape, decides whether to stay at the current size or toggle, and taps at the right moment. Successful navigation yields points and the tunnel continues, creating a fast‑paced decision‑making rhythm.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Narrow gaps** that only a small dot can pass; a large dot collides.
- **Wide barriers** that block a small dot, requiring the player to be large.
- **Moving spikes** that sweep across the tunnel while maintaining a fixed size requirement.
- **Sudden turns** that change the tunnel’s orientation, forcing rapid size toggles.

## Score
Score increases each time the player successfully passes through a segment of the tunnel (i.e., survives a gap or barrier). Bonus points are awarded for chaining multiple correct size toggles without mistake.

## Win and lose
The player loses when the dot collides with a barrier that does not match its current radius; the screen flashes, “Game Over” appears, and the final score is displayed. The player wins after traveling a predefined distance (e.g., completing 30 tunnel segments) where an “You Win!” overlay celebrates the achievement.

## Feel
A steady yet escalating pace with neon‑glow aesthetics against a dark background; each toggle creates a crisp visual “pop” and a subtle audio cue, reinforcing the rhythm of the tunnel. The experience feels like a reflex‑driven syncopated dance.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of instantly switching the dot’s size to thread impossibly tight gaps or block looming walls creates a quick, repeatable “aha!” moment that makes players both smile and curse in equal measure.
