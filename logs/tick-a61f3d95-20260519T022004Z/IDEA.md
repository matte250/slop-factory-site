# Random HTML Canvas Game Idea

**Title:** Asteroid Escape

**Concept:** The player pilots a small spaceship navigating an endless field of moving asteroids. The ship can thrust in four directions and fire a short-range laser to temporarily clear nearby asteroids. The goal is to survive as long as possible while collecting floating energy orbs to increase the score.

**Lose Condition:** The game ends when the ship collides with an asteroid or runs out of fuel (fuel depletes over time and is replenished by collecting energy orbs).
