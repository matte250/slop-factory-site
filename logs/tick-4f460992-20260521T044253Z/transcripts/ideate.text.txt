**Game Idea (saved to `IDEA.md`):**  

- **Title:** Canvas Sprint  
- **Genre:** Endless runner on HTML5 canvas.  
- **Core Loop:** Player controls a square that jumps (tap/space) to avoid scrolling rectangular obstacles.  
- **Loss Condition:** Collision with an obstacle or falling off the ground ends the game.  
- **Scoring:** Increments with distance traveled.
