# Game Idea: Meteor Dodge

**Concept**: The player controls a small spaceship that can move left and right along the bottom of the canvas. Meteors fall from the top at varying speeds.

**Goal**: Survive as long as possible while dodging meteors. Collect occasional power‑ups that grant temporary shields or speed boosts.

**Lose Condition**: The game ends immediately when a meteor collides with the spaceship.

**Core Mechanics**:
- Arrow keys or `A`/`D` for horizontal movement.
- Simple particle effects for meteor impacts.
- Score based on time survived and power‑ups collected.
- Optional difficulty scaling: meteors fall faster over time.
