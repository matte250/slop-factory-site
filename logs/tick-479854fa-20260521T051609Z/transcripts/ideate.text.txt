**Game Idea – Pixel Asteroid Dodge**

- **Genre:** Minimalist endless runner / dodge game.  
- **Core Loop:** The player controls a tiny pixel ship moving continuously forward. Asteroids spawn from the right, moving left at increasing speed. The ship can move up/down to avoid collisions.  
- **Lose Condition:** Collision with any asteroid ends the game.  
- **Goal:** Survive as long as possible; score equals time survived (or distance traveled).  

The idea is concise, feasible with plain HTML canvas, and includes a clear lose condition.
