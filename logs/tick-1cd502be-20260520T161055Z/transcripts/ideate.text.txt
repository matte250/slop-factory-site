# Game Idea: Asteroid Miner

Control a small mining ship that drifts through an endless field of moving asteroids. The ship automatically mines any asteroid it touches, gaining points. The player must steer to avoid collisions. **Lose condition:** The ship crashes into an asteroid or its fuel depletes.

The concept is simple enough to implement entirely with an HTML canvas, using basic graphics, physics, and scoring.
