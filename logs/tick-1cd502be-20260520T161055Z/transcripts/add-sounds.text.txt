Added audio support:

- Loaded thrust, mining, crash, and background music assets.
- Played background music on start (looped, low volume).
- Managed thrust sound: plays while the up‑arrow is pressed, stops otherwise.
- Played a mining sound when a low‑speed asteroid is harvested.
- Played a crash sound on impact at high speed.

All sound handling is integrated into the existing game loop without altering gameplay logic.
