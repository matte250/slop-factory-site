Added audio feedback:

- Loaded three `Audio` objects (jump, game‑over, optional background music) at the top of the script.
- Played `jumpSound` on each successful jump.
- Played `gameOverSound` when a collision ends the game and stopped background music.
- Background music is set to loop with low volume and can be enabled by uncommenting `bgMusic.play();`.

All changes are in **game.js**, preserving the existing canvas target (`id="game"`). The game now includes sound effects for a richer experience.
