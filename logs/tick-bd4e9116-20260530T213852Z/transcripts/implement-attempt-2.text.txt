Implemented **game.js** with a concise “Pixel Storm” canvas game:

- Targets the `<canvas id="game">` element.
- Handles ship movement (left/right arrows) and firing an EMP (space bar).
- Generates falling pixel blocks, detects collisions, and ends the game on impact.
- Renders ship, blocks, EMP effect, and a game‑over overlay.
- Uses requestAnimationFrame for smooth animation.
