Implemented richer graphics:

* **Background:** Black fill with a field of 100 white stars that regenerate on canvas resize.  
* **Planet:** Radial gradient (`#3aa` → `#113`) created on each resize.  
* **Ship:** Rendered as a green triangle pointing in the direction of its current velocity.  
* **Asteroids:** Each asteroid now uses its own radial gray gradient for a 3‑D look.  

All changes are in `game.js`; the game now looks and feels more polished while preserving the original mechanics.
