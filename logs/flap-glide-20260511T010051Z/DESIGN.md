## Concept
The player pilots a constantly moving dot, using taps to give it brief upward thrust while gravity pulls it down, steering through gaps in an endless series of scrolling pipes.

## Player
Avatar: a simple glowing dot that automatically moves forward. Verbs: tap (or click) to thrust upward, letting the dot rise briefly; otherwise it falls under gravity.

## Core loop
Every moment the screen scrolls left as new pipe segments appear with vertical gaps. The player watches upcoming gaps, taps to lift the dot at the right time, and releases to let it drop, continuously adjusting altitude to thread each gap. The tension of timing each thrust against the scrolling world creates a fast, reflex‑driven loop.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- Scrolling pipes: vertical columns with a single gap that move leftward at a steady pace. Colliding with any pipe ends the game.
- Edge of screen: falling below the bottom or flying above the top of the canvas triggers death.
- Moving shards (optional): occasional floating obstacles that drift with the scroll, adding brief surprise challenges.

## Score
The score increments by one each time the dot successfully passes through a pipe’s gap. Bonus points may be awarded for consecutive pipe passes without a miss.

## Win and lose
Lose: collision with a pipe or leaving the visible play area. The screen flashes, displays “Game Over” and the final score.
Win: reach a high‑score threshold (e.g., a set number of pipes passed) or survive for a set duration, then the screen celebrates with a “You Win!” overlay.

## Feel
Frantic, rhythm‑driven arcade action with a neon‑glow aesthetic. The dark background contrasts against a bright, pulsing dot and colorful pipe silhouettes. Minimalist sound effects punctuate each thrust and collision, reinforcing the fast‑paced loop.

## Why it's fun
The pure, moment‑to‑moment challenge of timing each tap to slip through ever‑tighter gaps creates an addictive “just one more try” thrill.
