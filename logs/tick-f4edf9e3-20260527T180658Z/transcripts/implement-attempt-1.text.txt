Implemented **Neon Grid Runner** in `game.js`. The script:

* Targets `<canvas id="game">`.
* Sets up canvas dimensions and neon‑styled grid.
* Handles left/right keyboard and click/tap controls to shift lanes.
* Spawns random obstacles, updates positions, checks collisions, and tracks score.
* Renders player, obstacles, grid, score, and a game‑over overlay.
* Runs a requestAnimationFrame loop for smooth animation.
