Added a simple audio system:

* **AudioContext** with a `beep(freq, dur)` helper for short sound effects.  
* **Ambient hum** playing continuously (low‑frequency sine).  
* **Lane‑change** triggers a 440 Hz beep.  
* **Collision** triggers a deeper 150 Hz beep.  
* Auto‑resume the context on the first user interaction.

All sounds are generated in‑code, no external assets required. The game now provides auditory feedback for player actions and a constant background tone.
