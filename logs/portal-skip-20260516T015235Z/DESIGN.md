## Concept
The player taps to place a short‑range portal ahead of a constantly moving dot; when the dot reaches the portal it instantly jumps forward, skipping any obstacles in between, with a cooldown that forces strategic timing.

## Player
A small glowing dot that automatically travels forward. The player can **place** a portal by tapping/clicking, which creates a temporary gateway a short distance ahead. The dot also **moves** forward continuously and **waits** while the portal cooldown is active.

## Core loop
Obstacles stream toward the dot as it advances. The player watches the upcoming section, decides whether to tap and create a portal, then watches the dot either collide with obstacles or enter the portal and teleport forward. After each portal use a cooldown period begins, during which the player must navigate the remaining hazards until the cooldown expires and another portal can be placed.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Static spikes** that sit on the path and instantly kill the dot on contact.
- **Moving sweepers** that slide horizontally or vertically, their motion telegraphed by a glowing trail.
- **Rotating lasers** that pivot around a fixed point, creating a rotating danger zone.
- **Falling blocks** that drop from the top, landing in the dot’s lane after a short delay.
Each hazard occupies a segment of the lane and can be bypassed only by timing a portal correctly.

## Score
Score increments each time the dot successfully avoids an obstacle, whether by passing it normally or by teleporting past it with a portal. Additional points are awarded for each portal use that skips multiple hazards in one jump.

## Win and lose
The player loses when the dot contacts any hazard while the portal is unavailable. The player wins by reaching a predefined distance or surviving a set number of obstacle waves, at which point a celebratory overlay appears.

## Feel
Frantic and high‑energy, with a neon‑lit aesthetic that glows against a dark background. The soundtrack consists of rapid chiptune arpeggios and short beeps marking portal placements and collisions, reinforcing the fast‑paced, risky feel.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of perfectly timing a portal to zip past a cluster of dangers gives an instant, satisfying “whoosh” moment that rewards quick reflexes and strategic foresight.
