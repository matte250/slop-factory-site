**Title:** Nebula Runner

- **Genre:** Endless runner (top‑down view)
- **Core loop:** The player steers a small spacecraft with arrow keys, navigating an ever‑scrolling nebula while dodging moving asteroids and enemy drones.
- **Mechanics:** Collect glowing energy orbs to boost speed and recharge a shield; press space to perform a short dash (cooldown 1 s).
- **Lose condition:** The ship’s health drops to zero after colliding with an asteroid or drone, or after three missed dashes when the shield is depleted.

The game can be implemented entirely with the HTML `<canvas>` API and simple JavaScript for input, rendering, and collision detection.

  // game state flag for game over sound

  function update(dt){

    if(keys.ArrowLeft) player.vx=-player.speed;
    if(keys.ArrowRight) player.vx=player.speed;
    if(keys.ArrowUp) player.vy=-player.speed;
    if(keys.ArrowDown) player.vy=player.speed;
    player.x+=player.vx; player.y+=player.vy;
