**Game Idea: Asteroid Escape**  
- **Genre**: Arcade / endless runner.  
- **Controls**: Arrow keys or mouse drag to move the ship.  
- **Gameplay**: The ship travels forward while asteroids drift across the HTML canvas. Dodge them to stay alive.  
- **Lose condition**: Any collision with an asteroid ends the game.  
- **Goal**: Survive as long as possible and rack up a high score based on distance or number of asteroids avoided.
