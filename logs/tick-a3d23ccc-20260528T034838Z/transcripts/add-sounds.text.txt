Added audio support:

- Initialized an `AudioContext` and resumed it on the first key press.  
- Implemented `playLaser()` for a short laser tone and `playExplosion()` for a noise‑based explosion effect.  
- Triggered `initAudio()` on any keydown to ensure the context is active.  
- Called `playLaser()` when the space bar fires the laser.  

The game now includes sound effects for laser firing and asteroid explosions.
