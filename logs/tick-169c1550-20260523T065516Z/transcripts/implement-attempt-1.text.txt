Implemented **Canvas Escape** game in `game.js`:

- Targets `<canvas id="game">`.
- Generates a random maze (recursive back‑tracker) on a 400×400 canvas.
- Controls: Arrow keys move a small red circle.
- Exit portal (green square) at maze’s opposite corner.
- 30‑second timer; losing when touching a wall or timeout, winning on reaching exit.
- Handles input, rendering, collision, timer, and win/lose alerts.
