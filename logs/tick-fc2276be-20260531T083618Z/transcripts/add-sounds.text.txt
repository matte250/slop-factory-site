Implemented sound effects:

* Added an `AudioContext` with a reusable `playFreq(freq, duration)` helper.
* Played a short “block” tone when an asteroid is intercepted by the shield.
* Played a lower‑pitch “hit” tone when an asteroid strikes the planet.
* Played a longer “game‑over” tone once when health reaches zero.
* Included a one‑time click listener to resume the audio context on the first user interaction (required for browsers that block autoplay).
