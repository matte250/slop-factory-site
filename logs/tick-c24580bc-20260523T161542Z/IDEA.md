# Game Idea: **Astro Dash**

A top‑down endless‑runner on an HTML canvas where you control a tiny spacecraft 
that constantly accelerates forward through a scrolling asteroid field. 
Use the mouse or arrow keys to steer left/right and a boost button for short 
speed bursts. Collect glowing energy orbs to extend your fuel and gain 
temporary shields.

**Lose condition**: The game ends when the ship collides with an asteroid 
or runs out of fuel.

The simple graphics (circles for asteroids, a triangle for the ship) and 
basic physics make it quick to prototype, while the lose condition adds a 
clear challenge and encourages replayability.
