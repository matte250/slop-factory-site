Implemented richer graphics:

- **Background**: Dark space with a static starfield.
- **Ship**: Drawn as a triangle with a blue‑gradient fill.
- **Hook**: Rendered as a white line with a circular tip.
- **Debris**: Normal junk as gray rectangles; dangerous asteroids drawn as rough red polygons for added visual variety.
- Added helper functions `initStars` and `drawStars` for starfield generation.

All drawing updates are now in the `draw()` function, preserving existing game logic.
