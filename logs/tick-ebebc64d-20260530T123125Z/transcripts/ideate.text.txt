# Game Idea: Space Junk Collector
- **Genre:** Arcade / Action  
- **Core mechanic:** Player moves a ship horizontally at the bottom and fires a grappling hook to capture falling space junk.  
- **Objective:** Collect as many junk pieces as possible before time runs out.  
- **Lose condition:** Ship is hit by an asteroid or any junk reaches the bottom.  
- **Key features:** Simple physics, increasing debris speed, score multipliers for combos.
