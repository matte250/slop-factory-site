# Game Idea: Space Junk Collector

- **Genre:** Arcade / Action
- **Core mechanic:** Player moves a small ship horizontally at the bottom of the canvas and fires a grappling hook to capture falling space junk.
- **Objective:** Collect as many pieces of junk as possible before the timer runs out.
- **Lose condition:** The ship is hit by an asteroid or any junk reaches the bottom of the screen.
- **Key features:** Simple physics, progressively faster debris, score multiplier for combos.
