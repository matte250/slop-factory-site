# Random Canvas Game Idea

**Title:** Neon Escape

**Concept:** Guide a neon-colored square through an endless scrolling field of moving barriers. Use arrow keys (or WASD) to shift left/right and jump over gaps. Barriers are rotating rectangles with gaps; colliding with a barrier or falling off the screen ends the game.

**Lose Condition:** The player loses if the square collides with a barrier or falls below the bottom edge.

**Why Canvas:** Simple shapes and animations can be drawn and updated at 60 fps using the HTML5 `<canvas>` API, making it lightweight and easy to implement.
