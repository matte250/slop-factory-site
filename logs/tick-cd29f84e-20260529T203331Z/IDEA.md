# Game Idea: Cosmic Courier

In **Cosmic Courier**, the player pilots a small delivery ship across a scrolling starfield. Asteroids and enemy drones drift in from the right. The ship can thrust up/down and fire a limited laser. Collecting cargo pods adds points, but colliding with any obstacle or letting the laser run out ends the game.

**Lose Condition:** Collision with an asteroid or enemy, or exhausting the laser energy.
