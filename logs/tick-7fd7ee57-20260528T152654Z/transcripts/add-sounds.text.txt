Added basic sound effects using the Web Audio API:

* **Jump** – a short 440 Hz beep when the player jumps (space bar or canvas click).  
* **Game‑over** – a lower 150 Hz beep when a collision ends the game.  

Implemented an `audioCtx` with a helper `playBeep(freq, dur)` and integrated it into the existing input handling and the game‑over routine. No other functionality was changed.
