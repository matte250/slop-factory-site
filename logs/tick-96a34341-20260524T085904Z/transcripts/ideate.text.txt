# Game Idea: **Orbit Dodge**

A minimalist HTML canvas game where the player controls a small ship orbiting a planet. Asteroids fly inward from the edges. The ship can rotate around the planet and thrust outward to dodge. Collecting power‑ups expands the orbit radius for more maneuverability.

**Lose condition:** colliding with an asteroid or drifting too close to the planet’s surface ends the game.
