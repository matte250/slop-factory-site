## Concept
The player guides a glowing dot that can temporarily split into two synchronized copies. By timing the split, each copy threads through offset gaps in incoming barriers, then automatically re‑merges to continue forward.

## Player
A single neon dot that drifts forward. The player can move it laterally, tap/click to split the dot into two parallel copies, and let the split automatically re‑merge. The split and merge are the core verbs alongside lateral movement.

## Core loop
The world scrolls toward the dot continuously. Walls with two staggered openings approach. The player moves left or right to align the dot (or its twins) with the openings, optionally tapping to split so each copy can occupy its own gap. After passing the wall the twins drift back together, and the next wall arrives. Each decision—move, split, or stay unified—feeds the moment‑to‑moment tension.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Offset‑gap walls**: Solid barriers spanning the screen except for two narrow openings that are horizontally offset. Only a split dot can use both openings simultaneously.
- **Sweeping lasers**: Thin moving beams that sweep vertically or horizontally, requiring precise timing to avoid; they cannot be bypassed by splitting, so they force careful positioning.
- **Rotating spikes**: Obstacles that rotate around a pivot, opening a brief passage that aligns with one gap; the player must split at the right moment to pass safely.

## Score
Score rises each time a wall is successfully cleared. Bonus points are awarded when a split is used to pass through both offset openings on a single wall. Additional points can be earned for narrowly avoiding sweeping lasers.

## Win and lose
The player loses instantly if any part of the dot (single or split) contacts a solid barrier or laser. The player wins after navigating a final "lock" wall that requires a successful split and precise alignment, signaling completion of the challenge. When the game ends, a clear overlay shows "Game Over" or "You Win!" with the final score.

## Feel
A fast‑paced, pulse‑driven rhythm set against a dark backdrop with a single bright dot. Neon glows and crisp synth beeps emphasize each split, merge, and near‑miss, creating an exhilarating blend of reflex and puzzle. The experience feels tight and responsive, rewarding precise timing.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of splitting the dot at just the right instant to slip each half through its own gap delivers a satisfying, almost cinematic moment of precision and control.
