Implemented sound effects:

- Created an `AudioContext` and `playTone` helper to generate simple synth tones.
- Added jump sound (300 Hz, 0.1 s) when the player jumps.
- Added collision sound (100 Hz, 0.3 s) when the player hits an obstacle.

All changes are in `game.js`, which targets the existing `<canvas id="game">`. The game now includes audio feedback for key actions.
