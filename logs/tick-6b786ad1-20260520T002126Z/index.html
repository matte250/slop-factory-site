# Asteroid Dodger

**Concept:** Control a ship that moves left/right at the bottom of the canvas and fires upward to destroy falling asteroids. Earn points for each asteroid destroyed and try to survive as long as possible.

**Lose condition:** The game ends when an asteroid touches the ship or the player runs out of lives.
