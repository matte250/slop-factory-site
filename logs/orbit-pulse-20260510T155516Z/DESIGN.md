## Concept
A pulsating dot orbits a moving nucleus; tapping sends out a radial pulse that temporarily expands the orbit, letting the dot slip through outer obstacles before the orbit contracts again.

## Player
The player controls the orbiting dot, visually a glowing point circling a central nucleus. Verbs: orbit, pulse (tap to trigger expansion), dodge (avoid obstacles by timing pulses).

## Core loop
Each moment the dot circles the nucleus while obstacles approach from the periphery. The player watches the pattern of incoming obstacles, decides the right instant to tap, and releases a pulse that widens the orbit, creating a brief gap to pass safely. After the pulse fades, the orbit shrinks and the cycle repeats, demanding rhythmic timing and anticipation.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Sweeping barriers**: elongated walls that rotate around the nucleus, sweeping across the orbit path. They kill the dot on contact.
- **Pulsing rings**: expanding rings that contract after a delay; the dot must slip through the shrinking gap.
- **Spinning shards**: fast‑rotating spikes that orbit at a larger radius; they become passable only when the orbit is expanded.
- **Static mines**: fixed obstacles that detonate if the dot lingers too long nearby, encouraging forward momentum.

## Score
- Successfully passing through a barrier during a pulse.
- Collecting floating energy orbs that appear after a pulse fades.
- Completing a sequence of consecutive pulses without collision (combo bonus).
- Clearing a wave of obstacles (survival bonus).

## Win and lose
Win: Survive a set number of waves or achieve a target score, triggering a spectacular outward burst as the nucleus explodes into light.
Lose: The dot contacts any hazard while the orbit is at its normal size or during a pulse, resulting in a shattering animation and a brief replay of the last successful pulse.

## Feel
A rhythmic, kinetic experience with a steady yet escalating tempo, rendered in neon‑glow visuals against a dark, minimal background. Audio consists of pulsing synth tones that swell with each tap, punctuated by crisp impact sounds on collisions, creating an arcade‑style auditory feedback loop.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of perfectly timed pulses that make the dot dance through impossible‑looking gaps, turning tension into a satisfying release.
