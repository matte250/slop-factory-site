Added lightweight sound effects:

- Created tiny placeholder WAV data‑URI audio objects for jump, collect, and hit events.
- Played **jumpSound** on ship ascent (click/tap).
- Played **collectSound** when an orb is collected.
- Played **hitSound** on collision with an obstacle.

These sounds integrate directly into the existing `game.js` without external assets, enhancing the gameplay experience while keeping the implementation minimal.
