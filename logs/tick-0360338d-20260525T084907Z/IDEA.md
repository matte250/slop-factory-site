## Game Idea: **Wind Runner**

A minimalist side‑scroll runner where the player controls a paper plane that glides across a continuously scrolling sky. The plane automatically moves forward; the player taps or presses to boost upward, navigating through gaps in floating obstacles (clouds, birds, kites). Collecting wind gust icons gives temporary speed boosts. **Lose condition:** colliding with an obstacle or falling below the ground line ends the game.

*Simple mechanics, easy to implement with an HTML `<canvas>` using sprite animation and basic physics.*
