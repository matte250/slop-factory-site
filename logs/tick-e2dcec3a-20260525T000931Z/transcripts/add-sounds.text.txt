Added simple sound effects using the Web Audio API:

* **Audio context** created at initialization.
* `playDash()` – short 600 Hz tone when the player dashes left/right.  
* `playCrash()` – lower 150 Hz tone on collision/game‑over.
* Resumes the audio context on the first click (required for browsers that start it in a suspended state).  

The new sounds are triggered in the click handler and when a crash occurs, keeping the visual‑only game logic unchanged.
