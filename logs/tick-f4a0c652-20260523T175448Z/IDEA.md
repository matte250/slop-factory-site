# Game Idea: Neon Runner

- **Core mechanic**: A neon dot continuously runs forward on a scrolling grid. The player taps/clicks to make the dot jump over or slide under incoming obstacles.
- **Goal**: Collect glowing shards for points while maintaining momentum.
- **Lose condition**: The game ends after the dot collides with obstacles three times or falls off the grid.
- **Canvas suitability**: Simple 2D shapes, smooth animation, and collision detection make it ideal for an HTML canvas implementation.
