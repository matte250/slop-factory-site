# Game Idea: Pixel Dodge

- **Genre:** Endless dodge‑avoidance.
- **Goal:** Survive as long as possible while the score counts upward.
- **Controls:** Arrow keys (or A/D) to move the player square left/right at the bottom of the canvas.
- **Mechanic:** Randomly generated circles fall from the top at increasing speed.
- **Lose Condition:** Collision between the player square and any falling circle ends the game.
