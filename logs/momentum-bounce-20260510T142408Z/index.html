## Concept
The player rolls a dot forward, building momentum with each tap and releasing it to bounce, flipping vertical direction while keeping speed, timing bounces to thread through alternating ceiling‑and‑floor gaps.

## Player
The avatar is a simple glowing dot that rolls along a horizontal conduit. It can –
- Tap to increase forward momentum (accelerate)
- Release tap to trigger a vertical bounce that flips its direction (up ↔ down) while preserving speed.

## Core loop
Every moment the dot rolls forward; the player watches the upcoming gap pattern, decides whether to keep building speed or release a bounce, and taps accordingly. The cycle of building momentum, releasing a bounce, and navigating the next gap repeats roughly every 1–3 seconds, creating a rhythm of anticipation and reaction.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Ceiling / Floor Gaps** – Alternating openings that require precise timing of bounces to pass.
- **Moving Barriers** – Horizontal spikes that sweep across the conduit, briefly blocking the path; they telegraph their movement before appearing.
- **Speed‑Drain Zones** – Sections that reduce accumulated momentum if the dot rolls through them without bouncing, forcing the player to re‑accelerate.

## Score
- Each successful bounce through a gap awards points.
- Bonus points for consecutive bounces without missing a gap (combo).
- Collectible “momentum orbs” placed in hard‑to‑reach spots grant extra points.

## Win and lose
- **Win**: Reach the end of the level, marked by a glowing portal that opens when the dot arrives with sufficient momentum.
- **Lose**: Crash into a ceiling or floor wall, or be hit by a moving barrier. The screen flashes and the dot shatters, followed by a “Game Over” overlay.

## Feel
Fast‑paced and kinetic, with a neon‑glow aesthetic that emphasizes motion. The visual style is sleek monochrome with neon accents, complemented by rhythmic chiptune pulses that sync with each bounce.

## Why it's fun
The thrill of timing a perfect bounce that flips direction while preserving speed creates a satisfying “whoosh” moment that feels both skillful and exhilarating.
