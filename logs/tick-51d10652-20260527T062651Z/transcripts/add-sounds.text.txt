Implemented sound effects:

- **Audio context** initialized and resumed on the first user interaction (click or key press).  
- **`playTone`** helper creates short sine‑wave tones.  
- Played a low‑frequency tone when the player collides with a circle (game over).  
- Played a higher‑frequency tone each time a new falling circle spawns.  

All audio handling is integrated into `game.js` without affecting existing gameplay. Enjoy the enhanced auditory feedback!
