# Game Idea: Cosmic Dodger

- **Genre:** Endless arcade shooter
- **Gameplay:** The player controls a spaceship that constantly moves forward across a star‑field background. Obstacles (asteroids, enemy drones) spawn from the right and move leftwards.
- **Controls:** Arrow keys (or WASD) to move up/down/left/right; space bar to fire a laser.
- **Goal:** Survive as long as possible while shooting obstacles and collecting floating stars for points.
- **Lose Condition:** Collision with any obstacle ends the game; the player’s ship is destroyed.
- **Visuals:** Simple shapes and pixel art rendered on an HTML `<canvas>`; background scrolls to convey motion.
- **Scoring:** Time survived plus collected stars, displayed on‑screen.
- **Extension Ideas:** Power‑ups, increasing speed, high‑score leaderboard.
