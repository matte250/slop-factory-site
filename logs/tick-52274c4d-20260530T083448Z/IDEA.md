# Canvas Game Idea: Meteor Dodge

- **Genre**: Arcade / Endless runner
- **Core loop**: Player controls a spaceship that moves left/right at the bottom of the canvas. Meteors fall from the top at increasing speed.
- **Controls**: Arrow keys or mouse/touch to move horizontally.
- **Goal**: Survive as long as possible, earning points for each meteor avoided.
- **Lose condition**: Collision with any meteor ends the game.
- **Visuals**: Simple shapes (triangular ship, circular meteors) with particle effects on collision.
- **Extensions**: Power‑ups (shield, slow‑motion) and difficulty scaling.
