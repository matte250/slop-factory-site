# Game Idea: Starfall Dodger

**Concept**: An endless runner on an HTML5 canvas where the player pilots a small spaceship that constantly moves forward.

**Controls**: Tap or click to thrust upward against a constant downward pull (gravity). Releasing the input lets the ship drift down.

**Obstacles**: Randomly generated asteroids and laser beams appear from the right. The ship must dodge them.

**Lose Condition**: The game ends when the ship collides with any obstacle **or** falls off the bottom edge of the canvas.

**Scoring**: Distance traveled (or time survived) increases the score.

**Why Canvas?**: Simple 2‑D sprite rendering and collision detection fit perfectly on the canvas.
