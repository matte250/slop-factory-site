# Game Idea: Neon Runner

- **Genre:** Endless runner on HTML canvas
- **Core Loop:** Player controls a square that automatically moves forward across a scrolling neon tunnel. Use space to jump over gaps or press down to slide under low barriers.
- **Obstacles:** Gaps, low ceilings, moving laser beams.
- **Lose Condition:** Colliding with any obstacle or falling into a gap ends the game.
- **Visuals:** Simple neon outlines on dark background, drawn with basic canvas shapes.
- **Goal:** Survive as long as possible; score based on distance traveled.
