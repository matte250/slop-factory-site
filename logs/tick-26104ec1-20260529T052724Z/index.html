# Game Idea: Orbit Escape

**Concept**: Pilot a small spacecraft around a rotating planet on an HTML canvas. Collect fuel cells while dodging space debris and resisting the planet’s gravity.

**Controls**: Arrow keys – rotate ship and thrust forward.

**Lose Condition**: The game ends when the ship runs out of fuel **or** collides with debris.

**Goal**: Survive as long as possible and achieve the highest score by gathering fuel cells.
