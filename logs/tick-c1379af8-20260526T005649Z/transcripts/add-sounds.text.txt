Implemented audio for **Pixel Runner**:

* Added a lightweight Web Audio API setup (`audioCtx`) with helper `playSound`.
* Created dedicated `playJumpSound` and `playHitSound` functions.
* Integrated jump sound in the `jump` handler.
* Triggered hit sound when a collision ends the game.

The game now plays a short tone on jumps and a lower‑frequency tone on collisions, enhancing feedback without external assets. Load `game.js` with the existing `<canvas id="game">` to experience the new sound effects.
