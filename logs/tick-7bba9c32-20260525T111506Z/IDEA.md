# Game Idea: Meteor Shield

- **Genre**: Arcade reflex
- **Canvas**: 2D top‑down view; meteors spawn at random positions at the top and fall down.
- **Player**: A horizontal shield controlled with mouse or arrow keys at the bottom of the canvas.
- **Goal**: Block incoming meteors. Each meteor that reaches the bottom reduces a health bar.
- **Lose condition**: Health reaches zero (e.g., after 5 missed meteors).
