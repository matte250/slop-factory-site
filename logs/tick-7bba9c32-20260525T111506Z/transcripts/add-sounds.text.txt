Added audio feedback using the Web Audio API:

* **Audio context** (`audioCtx`) lazily created on first sound request.  
* **`playTone(freq, dur)`** – simple square‑wave tone generator used for sound effects.  
* **Hit sound** – plays a 440 Hz tone (0.1 s) whenever a meteor is blocked by the shield.  
* **Game‑over sound** – plays a low 150 Hz tone (0.5 s) right before the “Game Over” alert.  

The new code lives in `game.js` alongside the existing graphics enhancements and requires no external assets.
