Implemented richer graphics and sound effects:

* **Graphics**
  * Ship drawn as a green triangle.
  * Asteroids rendered as gray circles.
  * Fuel cells rendered as yellow diamonds.
  * Added moving background stars (dark gray dots).
* **Audio**
  * Created an `AudioContext` with helper `playTone`.
  * Added sound callbacks:
    * `boostSound` – plays on space‑bar press.
    * `collisionSound` – plays on ship‑asteroid collision.
    * `fuelSound` – plays when collecting a fuel cell.
    * `gameOverSound` – plays after a short delay on game over.
* **Gameplay**
  * Updated update loop to spawn and animate stars.
  * Integrated sound triggers in input handling and collision logic.

The game now has a more polished visual style and basic audio feedback while retaining the original mechanics.
