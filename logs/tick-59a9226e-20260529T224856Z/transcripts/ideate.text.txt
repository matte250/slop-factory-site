# Game Idea: Asteroid Dodge  

- Player controls a spaceship moving left/right at the canvas bottom.  
- Random asteroids fall from the top with varying speeds.  
- Colliding with an asteroid reduces a health bar.  
- **Lose condition:** health reaches zero.  
- Score is based on time survived or asteroids dodged.
