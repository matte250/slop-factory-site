Added sound effects:

* Initialized an `AudioContext` and helper `playTone` for simple tones.  
* Resumes the audio context on first user interaction (keydown or click).  
* Plays a low‑hum background loop.  
* Plays a collision tone when a meteor hits the ship.  
* Plays a short point tone whenever a meteor is dodged and the score increments.  

All changes are contained within `game.js` and keep the existing gameplay intact.
