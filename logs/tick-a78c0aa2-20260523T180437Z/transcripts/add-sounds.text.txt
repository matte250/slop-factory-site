Implemented sound effects:

- Created an `AudioContext` and helper `playTone(freq, duration)` for simple sine‑wave tones.
- Added a jump sound (300 Hz, 0.1 s) triggered on player jumps.
- Added a collision sound (100 Hz, 0.3 s) triggered when the player hits an obstacle.

The game now includes auditory feedback while preserving the enhanced graphics and core gameplay.
