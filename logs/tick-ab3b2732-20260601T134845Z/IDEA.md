# Canvas Game Idea: Meteor Shatter

- Player controls a small spaceship using arrow keys.
- The canvas scrolls vertically with meteors falling from the top.
- Click to fire a laser; meteors split into smaller fragments when hit.
- Collect points for each meteor destroyed.
- **Lose condition:** The spaceship is destroyed after three collisions with any meteor fragment.
- Simple graphics and physics make it quick to prototype on HTML canvas.
