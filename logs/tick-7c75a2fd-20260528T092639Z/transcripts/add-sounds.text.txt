Implemented audio feedback:

* **AudioContext** creation with resume handling on first key press or click.
* **playTone** helper for simple sine‑wave sounds.
* **Thrust sound** – plays once when thrust starts.
* **Collision sound** – triggers on ship‑asteroid impact.
* **Fuel pickup sound** – plays when fuel is collected.
* Added `prevThrusting` flag to detect thrust start and prevent continuous triggering.

All changes are in `game.js`; the game now includes basic sound effects while preserving the enhanced graphics. Enjoy the added audio!
