# Game Idea: Orbital Dodge

- **Genre**: Arcade / Action
- **Core mechanic**: Player controls a ship orbiting a planet, switching between inner and outer orbit (or moving radially) to dodge incoming asteroids that spawn from the edges and head toward the planet.
- **Lose condition**: Collision with any asteroid or running out of fuel (fuel depletes over time).
- **Goal**: Survive as long as possible, earning points for each asteroid avoided.
