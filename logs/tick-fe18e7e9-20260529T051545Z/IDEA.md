# Game Idea: Neon Reflex

**Concept**: The player controls a small neon square that automatically moves forward across a scrolling, neon‑styled tunnel. Randomly generated obstacles (bars, spikes, and moving walls) appear from the top and bottom. The player can tap/click to toggle the square's vertical position (up/down) to avoid them.

**Lose Condition**: The game ends when the square collides with any obstacle or the tunnel walls.

**Core Mechanics**:
- Simple tap or spacebar to switch lanes.
- Gradually increasing speed and obstacle density.
- Score based on distance traveled.

This concept fits well within a single HTML5 canvas and can be built with plain JavaScript.
