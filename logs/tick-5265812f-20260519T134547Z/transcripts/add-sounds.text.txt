Added audio cues:

* **Thrust** – continuous low‑frequency tone while the mouse is pressed, started/resumed on mousedown and stopped on mouseup/leave.  
* **Collision** – short burst when the ship hits an asteroid.  
* **Game‑over** – longer tone when health reaches zero.

Implemented an `AudioContext`, created oscillator‑based functions for each sound, and hooked them into the input and collision logic. The game now plays these sounds in sync with the visual actions.
