# Asteroid Escape

**Concept**: A top‑down HTML canvas game where the player pilots a small spaceship through an endless field of moving asteroids.

**Controls**: Tap/click to thrust forward and steer left/right.

**Goal**: Dodge asteroids and collect occasional power‑ups that restore health.

**Lose Condition**: The ship has a health bar; each collision reduces health. The game ends when health reaches zero.
