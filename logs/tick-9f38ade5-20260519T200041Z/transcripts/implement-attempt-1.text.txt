Implemented **game.js** with a concise Asteroid Dodge game targeting the `<canvas id="game">` element:

- Sets up canvas, ship, asteroid, and fuel handling.
- Arrow‑key controls for the ship.
- Random spawning of asteroids (collision ends game) and fuel cells (refill fuel).
- Fuel consumption mechanic; game ends when fuel depletes.
- Renders ship, obstacles, fuel bar, and “Game Over” message.
