## Concept
The player guides an ever‑forward scrolling dot that automatically moves through a tunnel. With each tap (or click) the dot toggles between a tiny and a bulky radius, allowing it to squeeze through narrow gaps or block wide openings, creating a rhythmic pattern of timing and size control.

## Player
A glowing dot that constantly advances forward. The player’s only direct action is to toggle the dot’s radius between two preset sizes (tiny and bulky). Optionally the player can steer slightly left or right to align with the tunnel’s center, but the core mechanic is the size toggle.

## Core loop
Every 1‑3 seconds a new tunnel segment scrolls into view, presenting a gap of a particular width. The player reads the upcoming gap, decides whether to stay tiny or become bulky, and taps to toggle size at the right moment. As the segment reaches the dot, a collision check determines success; a successful pass awards points and the tunnel continues. The loop repeats, with gap patterns gradually becoming more demanding.

## Hazards / Obstacles
- **Dynamic gaps:** Sections of the tunnel wall that open and close, creating narrow or wide passages that require the correct size.
- **Static spikes:** Fixed protrusions that fill part of the tunnel; they are harmless when the dot is tiny but become lethal if the dot is bulky and collides.
- **Moving shutters:** Sliding barriers that sweep horizontally, demanding precise timing of the size toggle and occasional lateral adjustment.

## Score
The score increments each time the player successfully passes through a gap. Bonus points are awarded for consecutive flawless passes (a combo) or for passing particularly tight gaps while in the bulky state.

## Win and lose
The player loses instantly when the dot collides with a wall or obstacle due to an incorrect size. The player wins by surviving a predefined distance (e.g., 500 tunnel segments) or by reaching a time limit (e.g., 2 minutes) without a collision, at which point a celebratory overlay appears.

## Feel
A steady, escalating rhythm that starts gentle and becomes tense, set against a dark neon‑lit tunnel. Visuals are high‑contrast with a pulsating neon glow, and the soundscape consists of subtle synth pulses that accent each toggle and successful pass. The experience feels meditative yet increasingly frantic as gaps tighten.

## Why it's fun
The sheer thrill of timing a size toggle to barely squeeze through a gap—right before a collision—delivers an instant, visceral “yes!” moment that makes players both smile and curse.
