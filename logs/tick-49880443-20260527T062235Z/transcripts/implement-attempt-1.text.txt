Implemented **`game.js`** with a concise, functional asteroid‑dodge game:

- Targets the `<canvas id="game">` element.
- Handles player movement via ArrowUp/ArrowDown.
- Spawns moving asteroids, detects collisions, and ends the game.
- Displays a simple score based on survival time.
- Includes background, ship drawing, and game‑over overlay.

Run the HTML page containing the canvas to play.
