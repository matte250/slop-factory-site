# Game Idea

**Title:** Canvas Dodge

**Concept:**  
- Player controls a circular avatar moving horizontally on an HTML canvas.  
- Obstacles (rectangular blocks) spawn from the right and travel leftward.  
- Use arrow keys or mouse to jump or slide and avoid collisions.  
- **Lose condition:** Avatar collides with an obstacle or falls off the bottom of the canvas.  
- Score increases over time based on survival duration.
