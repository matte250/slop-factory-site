# Game Idea: Pixel Escape

A simple endless runner where the player controls a pixel‑size square that automatically moves forward across a scrolling, procedurally‑generated terrain. The player can jump or slide to avoid obstacles (spikes, gaps, moving blocks). The canvas renders the terrain and obstacles in flat colors. The game ends (lose condition) when the square collides with any obstacle or falls into a gap. The score increases with distance traveled.
