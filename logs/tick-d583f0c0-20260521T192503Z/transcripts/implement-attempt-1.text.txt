Implemented **game.js** with a concise endless‑runner based on the IDEA.md description.  

- Targets `<canvas id="game">`, sets up rendering context.  
- Defines player movement (jump, slide) with gravity.  
- Generates procedural obstacles (spikes, gaps) and scrolls them.  
- Handles collision detection, scoring, and a simple “Game Over” overlay.  
- Adds keyboard controls: **Space** to jump, **ArrowDown** to slide.  

The script runs automatically when loaded, creating the requested game.
