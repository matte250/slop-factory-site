# Game Idea: Space Junk Escape

**Genre:** Endless runner / arcade
**Controls:** Arrow keys or WASD to steer a small spaceship.
**Goal:** Fly forward through an endless field of scrolling space debris, collect power‑ups for speed boosts.
**Lose Condition:** Colliding with any piece of debris reduces shields; when shields reach zero the ship is destroyed and the game ends.
**Why HTML Canvas?** Simple 2‑D sprites, continuous scrolling background, and collision detection are easy to implement on a canvas.
