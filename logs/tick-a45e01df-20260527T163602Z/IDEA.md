# Game Idea: "Tumble Tunnel"

- A ball constantly falls under gravity through a vertically scrolling tunnel of moving barrier bars.
- The player tilts left/right (keyboard or mouse) to guide the ball through gaps.
- Collect floating orbs for points while navigating.
- **Lose condition**: the ball collides with any barrier or falls out of the canvas bounds.
- Simple HTML canvas implementation: physics (gravity, collision), rendering of bars/orbs, input handling.
