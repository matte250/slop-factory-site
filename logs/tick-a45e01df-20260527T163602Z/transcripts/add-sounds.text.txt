Added sound effects:

* **Audio setup** – created an `AudioContext`, resumed on first key press.
* **Tone generator** – helper `playTone` with envelope; wrappers `playCollect`, `playCrash`, `playScore`.
* **Collision** – `playCrash()` when the ball hits a barrier.
* **Orb collection** – `playCollect()` when an orb is collected (score increment).

All changes are in **`game.js`**, enhancing the game with immediate audio feedback while preserving existing mechanics.
