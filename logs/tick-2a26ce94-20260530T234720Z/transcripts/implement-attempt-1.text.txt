Implemented **game.js** with a minimal “Meteor Shower Escape” canvas game:

- Targets the existing `<canvas id="game">`.
- Handles ship movement via arrow keys.
- Spawns meteors that increase in speed and frequency over time.
- Detects collisions, updates score, and shows a game‑over overlay.
- Includes simple background stars and drawing logic.

Add this script to the HTML page to activate the game.
