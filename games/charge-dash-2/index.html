Charge Dash

Hold to charge a kinetic burst; releasing sends the dot forward a short distance, phasing through obstacles, then a cooldown forces timing the next charge.

  • Hold Space (or mouse button) to charge, release to dash
  • Arrow Left/Right to aim
  • Press R or click Restart
